For the first time ever we are able to offer this tiny species of livebearer. Scolichthys iota has been discovered and described back in 1967 already, but has become never known alive to hobbyists until very recently. Our specimens are German bred.
Males attain a maximum length of only 1.5 cm, females get almost double of that length. The number of dark stripes on the flanks of the males varies individually.
For our customers: the fish have code 289803 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade. Available in very limited numbers only!
