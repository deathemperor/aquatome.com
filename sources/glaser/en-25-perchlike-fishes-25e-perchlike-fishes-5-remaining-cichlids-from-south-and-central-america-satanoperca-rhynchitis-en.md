We received beautiful German bred specimens of this rare eartheater. Until the species has been described in 2012 under the name of Satanoperca rhynchitis it was known in the hobby as Satanoperca sp. “Red Lips”. The fish develop the pretty red lips only when adult, so we add a picture of an adult specimen to this news.
For our customers: the fish have code 687633 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
