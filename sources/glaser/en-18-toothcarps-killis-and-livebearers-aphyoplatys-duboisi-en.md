It has been many, many years ago since we were able to import this charming dwarf killi the last time. Currently the fish are about 1.5-2 cm long, maximum length reported for Aphyoplatys duboisi is only 2.5 cm. The fish have been collected in the surroundings of Brazzaville.
Despite their very small size these fish are not at all very sensitive. In fact the males can be even a bit quarrelsome against each other. But they will not do real harm during combating. It is the best choice to keep these jewels in small tanks on their own. Here the keeper has the best control over them. The fish spawn readily, but to raise the youngsters is sometimes a bit tricky. A. duboisi is a substrate spawner and the eggs do not need a dry period to hatch.
These dwarf killis feed readily on dried food as well as on Artemia nauplia and comparative small food items. If one wants to breed the fish soft and slightly acidic water is recommended. The water level should be rather low, about 15 cm is best.
