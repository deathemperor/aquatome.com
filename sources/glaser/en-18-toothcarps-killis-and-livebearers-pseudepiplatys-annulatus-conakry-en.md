This tiny and charming little jewel becomes only 3-4 cm long. Our animals are wild collected specimens of the sport known in the hobby as the 'Conakry variety'. Read more on the species here: http://www.aquariumglaser.de/en/news/Pseudepiplatys_annulatus_en/
For our customers: the fish have code 322003 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
Lexikon: Pseudepiplatys: from ancient Greek, means “false Epiplatys”. annulatus: Latin, means “ringed”.
Common name: Clown Killi
