This week we were able to import a new sport of the Endler Guppy for the first time. Its bright contrasting yellow and black coloration will for sure excite the guppy enthusiasts. Regarding body shape and behaviour this new sport is a typical Endler Guppy (Poecilia wingei). This means it is a rather small fish (males may reach 2.5 cm, females 4 cm length) and very lively.
For our customers: the fish has code 419100 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
