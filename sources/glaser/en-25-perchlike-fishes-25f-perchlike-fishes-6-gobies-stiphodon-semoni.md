(23.Aug.2008) From Indonesia we received this beautiful neon-blue goby. Stiphodon semoni grows up to 4-5cm and was several times imported under the wrong name Sthiphodon elegans. Its habitats are fast floating hillstreams with rocky ground. It is found in Indonesia, the Philippines, New Guniea and the Solomon Islands. The nourishment for this item is easy: we feed it dry food and life Tubifex. All in all a very fabulous goby which should be kept by advanced hobbyists.(Photo F.Schäfer, Text R.Neunkirchen)
