The genus Sewellia has made the hillstream loach popular in the hobby. The splendid Sewellia lineolata is named also orante hillstream loach. However, there are more beautiful species in the genus which are imported now and then. Sadly it is often not possible to determine them exactly. We were able to import one of the latter species now.
In any case this ornate hillstream loach is a real enrichment for the aquarium hobby!
For our customers: the fish have code 455044 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
