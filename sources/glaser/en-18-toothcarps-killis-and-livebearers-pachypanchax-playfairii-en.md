Aquarists who like to read in elder books find this killifish in all literature until the 1970ies: the Golden Panchax, Pachypanchax playfairii. Then the species seems to have disappeared from the aquarium hobby mainstream. By the way: the Golden Panchax is the only primary freshwater fish species from the Seychelles.
The Golden Panchax belongs to the more robust species of killifish and is one of the comparatively rare killis that can be kept even in community tanks. However, tankmates should not be too small. The maximum length reported in literature is 10 cm; our specimens are currently about half that long and sexually fully mature. Females can be easily recognized by the dark spot in the dorsal fin. In specimens ready to spawn the scales on the back ruffle up. This phenomenon is unique among all the fish species known so far.
Anyway, we are glad to be able to offer that pretty oldtimer again. Obviously it has survived in the tanks of real enthusiasts, for the specimens in our fishhouse are tank-bred ones, bred in Europe…
