We were able to import a pretty species of squeaker from the Congo: Synodotis notatus. The species attains a maximum length of about 25 cm and is worth mentioning for at least two reasons: first, the number of spots on the flanks varies a lot individually. Most specimens have only one spot, but there are also some with two,three or even more sports. And twice, this lively and also day-active squeaker is one of the most peaceful species of the genus.
For our customers: the fish have code 183802 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
