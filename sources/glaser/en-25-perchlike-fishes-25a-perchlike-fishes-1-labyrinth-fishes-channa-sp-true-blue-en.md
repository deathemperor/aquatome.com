We could import this extreme beautiful species of snakehead in very small numbers from the north of India. The True Blue is obviously a close relative of C. stewartii; both attain a length of about 25 cm. The two photographed specimens have a quite different pattern. Most probably the smaller specimen is a female (it has 15.5 cm total length, the larger animal is 18 cm long).
The True Blue should be kept at least for periods of several weeks at rather cool water temperatures, eg 15-20°C, as it originates from mountain areas. The species has been bred already in the aquarium, it is a mouthbrooding species.
