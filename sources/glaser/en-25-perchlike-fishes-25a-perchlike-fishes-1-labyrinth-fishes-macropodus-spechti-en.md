The ancient homeland of the Black Paradise Fish (Macropodus spechti, formerly M. concolor) is Vietnam. We obtained now fully grown, wonderful specimens from there. The fish are most probably bred ones, but they differ clearly from the strain bred in Europe since the 1930ies. In direct comparison the new importations show a very attractive, brass coloured shine on the body and the ventral fins are extraordinary long.
For our customers: the fish have code 425604 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
