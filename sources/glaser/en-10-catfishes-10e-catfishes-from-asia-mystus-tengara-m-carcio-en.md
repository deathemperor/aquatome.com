We received the smallest of the striped catfish species from India, which were all called formerly “Mystus vittatus”. The small species also had to face a real mess regarding the correct naming. For a long time the small species was called M. tengara, currently most scientists believe that the correct name has to be M. carcio. May this be as it is: the charming catfish attains a maximum length of only 4-6 cm and is perfectly suited for the home aquarium.
