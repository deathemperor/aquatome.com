The development of new colour sports in dwarf shrimps still goes on and on. The latest brandnew sport reached us now from Taiwan under the name of Fantasy Jelly. This pretty animal is of a deep, intense royal blue; the colour is so intensive that the shrimps sometimes even look black. Possibly this new sport is a further stage of the “Sushi Blue” which we could introduce to you about one year ago (see http://www.aquariumglaser.de/en/fish-archive/lobster-shrimps-crabs-mussels-snails-en/Neocaridina_heteropoda_Sushi_Blue_en/)
