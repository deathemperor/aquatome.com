The breeders and farmers of ornamental fish in Sri Lanka still suffer from the catastrophal rains some weeks ago. Nevertheless they supply us with wonderful guppys and other livebearers, for example the beautiful Guppy Magenta Tuxedo. We have this strain from Sri Lank since 1998 . All customers that order now bred fish from Sri Lanka do not only order most beautiful and healthy fish, but also support the many families that need the income from breeding ornamentals and help them to rebuild their destroyed facilities.
For our customers: the Guppy Magenta Tuxedo has code 418503 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
