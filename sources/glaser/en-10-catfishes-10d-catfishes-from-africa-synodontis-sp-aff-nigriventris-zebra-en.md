We currently have a limited number of the very pretty and rare Synodontis sp. aff. nigriventris ZEBRA . The species differs from the “common” Upside Down Catfish by the extremely contrasting pattern. The species is – as far as it is known – restricted to the Mai Ndome region in the DR Congo. Size of our specimens: around 4 cm.
For our customers: the fish have code 183414 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
