For many years we obtained the striped headstander that looks like the big brother of the golden pencilfish only in single specimens. Now we were finally able to import about 30 specimens of the pretty fish from Peru. The species is a typical headstander in all respects of behaviour. It can become larger than 20 cm.
For our customers: the fish have code 205903 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
