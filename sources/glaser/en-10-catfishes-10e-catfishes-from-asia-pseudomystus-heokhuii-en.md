This pretty species of bumblebee catfish was discovered and scientifically described only in 2008. We could import the species now for the first time ever. P. heokhuii becomes about 6 cm long and is a very peaceful species of catfish.
Despite the fact that Pseudomystus heokhuii inhabits extreme blackwater habitats in the wild, where the pH is around 3.5 -4.5 they adapt very well to different water conditions. The females develop spawn and even in the phototank the male showed courtship behaviour. So it seems that this pretty bumblebee catfish will even breed in aquaria.
