There are a number of ringed species of Leporinus. Each of them has its own special charme. We obtained a shipment of very nice, small Leporinus affinis from Peru. Currently they are about 5 cm long, but fully grown they will reach up to 30 cm length. A closer look on the fish made clear that the larger part of the shipment was composed of another ringed species: Leporinus desmotes. This beautiful fish also reminds one in the Abramites species. L. desmotes stays much smaller than L. affinis and attains a maximum length of about 15-20 cm. It is also not so quarrelsome as its larger cousin.
