Every year we eagerly await the start of the season for wild collected discus. Now it has started finally and we received, for example, breathtaking beautiful Royal Blues from Oriximina (Rio Trombetas), among them show animals with a length of about 14-17 cm (see photos). But we also obtained smaller specimens, which are also very beautiful…
For our customers: the fish have code 731064 (8-12 cm), 731065 (12-15 cm), 731066, and 731076 (14-17 cm) on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
