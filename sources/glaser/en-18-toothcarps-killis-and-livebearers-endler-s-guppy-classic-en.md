In the meantime Poecilia wingei is accepted as a species on its own by most people working with guppys. However, the species itself is as variable as the Common Guppy, Poecilia reticulata. This fact is often overlooked. So the “classical” Endler´s Guppy represents already an artifical sport that is based on selective breeding. The wild population of the fish is much more variable in coloration. Nevertheless: the classical Endler´s Guppy is one of the most beautiful and lively guppys at all!
