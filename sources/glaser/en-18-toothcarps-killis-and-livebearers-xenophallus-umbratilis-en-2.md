Sadly this very attractive livebearer can be offered only very rarely. The species is also known as the “Golden Teddy” among hobbyists. Males become about 4 cm, females up to 6 cm long. Initially the species is native in Costa Rica, but the fish we have now are German bred ones.
For our customers: the fish have code 475802 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade. Available in small numbers only!
