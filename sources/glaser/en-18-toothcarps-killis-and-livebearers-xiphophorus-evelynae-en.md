This extremely seldom offered wild platy species is a close relative of X. variatus and has been described initially only as a subspecies of Variable Platyfish. X. evelynae lives exclusively in the Tecolutia River basin on the Atlantic slope of Mexico.
For our customers: the animals have code 476528 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
