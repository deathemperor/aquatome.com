We want to prolong our small news series on wild collected Corydoras from Peru once more; we simply have so many beautiful species ! And here we go:
Corydoras cf. sychri C97 Magnificent, large animals, available only very occasionally and in small numbers. Code 247024 on our stocklist.
Corydoras leucomelas An ideal cory for smaller community tanks; this species attains a maximum length of 4 cm only. Code 233503 (md) and 233504 (md-lg).
Corydoras arcuatus A real classic and simply beautiful. We have two sizes , among them some xlg-animals (photo). Code 222003 (md) and 222005 (xlg).
Corydoras sp. C91 This fish is scientifically still undescribed and a real rarity. Very attractive! Code 229603
