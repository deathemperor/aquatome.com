We received beautiful, fully grown, about 8 cm long specimens of this peaceful, mouthbrooding Betta as German bred. Keeping these fish is problem free, but one should know that Betta unimaculata is an excellent jumper, for it lives in brooks in the wild. So the tank must be covered carefully.
For our customers: the fish have code 392103 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
