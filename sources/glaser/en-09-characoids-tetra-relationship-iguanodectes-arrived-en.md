We can offer currently three of the extremely attractive, only very occasionally imported species of Iguanodectes. from Venezuela arrived Iguanodectes geisleri (see http://www.aquariumglaser.de/en/fish-archive/tetras-en/south-america-en/Iguanodectes_geisleri_en/) and I. adujai, and from Brazil we obtained a species that porbably represents I. purusii.
For our customers: the animals have code 264612 (I. geisleri), 264353 (I. adujai), and 264402 (I. cf. purusii) on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade. Available in very limited numbers only!
