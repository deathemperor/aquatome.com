Formerly the pike cichlids were splitted in two genera. Batrachops and Crenicichla. Batrachops were the rather blunt-headed species. Nowadays this classification is not accepted anymore and Batraochops seen only as a synonym of Crenicichla. However, hobbyists have not forgotten the old name…
One of these blunt headed species is Crenicichla semifasciata from Paraguay. The attractive species is sadly only very seldom available. The pictures are made from a specimen that reached us a by-catch of Crenicichla edithae directly from Paraguay, but we also have a few tank-bred specimens . These fish have reached sexually maturity. The parents of this aquarium strain originate from the Rio Tagatyia.
