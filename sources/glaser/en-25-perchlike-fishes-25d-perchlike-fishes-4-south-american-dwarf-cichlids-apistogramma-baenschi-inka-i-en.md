Apistogramma baenschi from Peru is for sure among the most beautiful species of dwarf cichlid. The male combines splendid colours (especially the wonderful caudal fin) with a fantastic high dorsal fin and the female is also a real eyecatcher due to its unique tiger pattern. Currently we have both wild collected and bred specimens (the photos are taken from our bred specimens).
For our customers: the fish have code 628813 (wild) and 628823 (bred) on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
