We can offer only very rarely wild collected fishes from the USA. But currently we have some species of minnow , among them the very pretty Chrosomus erythrogaster. These fish do not need a heater, but feel also very comfortable at temperatures over 24°C. Maximum size for that species is 10-12 cm, currently the fish are 5-7 cm long.
For our customers: the fish have code 411043 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade. Available in limited numbers only!
