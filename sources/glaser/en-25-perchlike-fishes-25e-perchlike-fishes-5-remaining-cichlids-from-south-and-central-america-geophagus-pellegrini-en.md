Sadly we can offer this beautiful mouthbrooding cichlid from Colombia only very rarely; the last time we had it was 5 years ago. Now we had the opportunity to import this animal. They originate from the Chocó region in Colombia, located in the West of the country. The main river of the Chocó region is the Rio Atrato.
The few specimens we imported now – and from which the photos were taken – are almost again, but we have the opportunity to order more specimens.
For our customers: the fish have code 680255 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
