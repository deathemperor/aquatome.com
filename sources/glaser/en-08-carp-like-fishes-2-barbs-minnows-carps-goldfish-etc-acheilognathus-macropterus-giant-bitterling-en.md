For the first time ever we obtained this splendid bitterling via Hongkong. The species is said to become more than 25 cm long and this would make it the largest species of bitterling at all! However, our fish are currently 6-8 cm long and in full colour, so maybe more than one species is involved, as the bitterlings are a very confusing group of fishes. In any case our new importations are a gorgeous supplement to the ornamental fishes from China.
