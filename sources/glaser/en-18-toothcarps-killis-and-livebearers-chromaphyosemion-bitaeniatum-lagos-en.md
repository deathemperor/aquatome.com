This beautiful killi is only very occasionally available as German bred. Currently we have some of these bred ones . They are descendants from the population from Lagos (Nigeria). Our fish are large, full in colour and very pretty. This species is suitable for community tanks with peaceful, small fish (barbs, tetras, catfish etc.).
For our customers: the fish have code 305303 on our stocklist. Please note that we exclusively supply the wholesale trade.
