The name means "adorned with pearls". The original habitat is small, shallow ponds no more than about 1 ft deep, fed by springs and seepage, with clear water and dense Elodea in open grassland. Its closest relative is Danio erythromicron.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Tiny, slender danio with a small pointed head. Fins: Orange-red fins with black bands on the dorsal, anal and pelvic fins. Coloration: Dark blue-gray to blue-green body covered in pearly gold-white spots. Males have a red-orange belly and brighter fins; females are duller with a fuller body. Habitat: Shallow, clear, densely planted spring-fed ponds. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful. Keep in groups of at least 6. Males spar and display at each other. They need a well-filtered tank with swimming space and hiding places such as plants.Max Size: About 0.8 in standard length (about 1 in total length)pH: 6.0 to 7.5Temperature: 72 to 79°FDiet: Feeds on small insect larvae, crustaceans and worms in the wild.
