IUCN Red List: N/A (this is a hybrid color form and does not occur in the wild) Origin: Captive bred Betta Mahachaiensis "Copper" Betta Mahachaiensis "Copper" is a hybrid color form of Betta mahachaiensis, a member of the Betta splendens complex described in 2012 from Samut Sakhon province in Thailand. Mahachais are known for their highly iridescent tail and the two bars on their cheek plates. The copper color comes from gray iridescence combined with the metallic gene, which gives a metallic copper shine in place of the wild green. They are hardy and easy to keep, which makes them a great choice for beginners. These are quality fish from our breeder in Vietnam. Genes for iridescence: blbl Genes for metallic: ++ Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Slender and elongated, typical of the Betta splendens complex, with a slightly compressed body. Reaches about 2 to 2.4 inches (5 to 6 cm). Tail Shape: Short, rounded caudal fin. Heavy iridescence extends into the tail, which is one of the traits mahachais are known for. Scaling: Metallic copper scales line up in rows, forming horizontal stripes along a dark body. Coloration: Dark brown to black base with a metallic copper to gold shine across the body and fins. Males are the most intense, especially when displaying. Females are duller and brownish. Operculum: Two parallel metallic copper vertical bars on a black opercular membrane with no red. Habitat (wild Betta mahachaiensis): Native to the coast of the Gulf of Thailand around Samut Sakhon. Lives in stagnant swamps, pools and ponds, often around nipa palms, in fresh to brackish water (1 to 10 ppt salinity) with a pH of about 6.9 to 7.8. Uses its labyrinth organ to breathe air. Care Guide Temperament: Males are VERY aggressive and should be kept alone or with non-male betta tankmates. Multiple females can be kept together in a planted community tank if there is enough space. Max Size: 2.3 inches TDS: 60 to 200 (we keep them in soft, low-TDS water to match our wild bettas, but they tolerate a wider range). Mahachais can live in brackish water, but we do not keep them in brackish conditions. pH: 6.0 to 7.5 Temperature: 75 to 85°F (much better coloration above 78°F). Diet: Carnivorous. We currently feed live blackworms, frozen bloodworms and pellets. Breeding: Bubblenester. The male builds a bubble nest under a floating leaf or plants and places the eggs in it after spawning. Remove the female once spawning is done. Eggs hatch in 24 to 48 hours, and the fry are free-swimming about 3 days later. Start them on infusoria, then move to baby brine shrimp.
