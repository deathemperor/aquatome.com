It lives in slow tributaries and floodplain waters with submerged grasses, roots and leaf litter. Unlike most corydoras, it spends much of its time in midwater, schooling in tight groups. Our fish are captive bred, so they handle a wider range of water and acclimate more easily than wild fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Tiny, slim armored catfish.
