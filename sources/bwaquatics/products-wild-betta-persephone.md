It is one of the smallest and darkest members of the coccina complex, named after Persephone, queen of the Underworld, for its near-black color. Much of its habitat has been drained for oil palm, and Malaysian hobbyists have bred the species and released it back into protected forest in Johor.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Small and slender, one of the smallest bettas. Fins: Rounded caudal fin and a long anal fin. Males have black pelvic fins with white filament tips. Scaling: Small, even scales.
