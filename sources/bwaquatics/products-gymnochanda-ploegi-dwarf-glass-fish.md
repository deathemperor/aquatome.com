Please proceed with caution, as this is one of the most difficult fish to keep.We did not take the photo; it is a placeholder and all credit goes to Aquarium Glaser.Gymnochanda ploegi (Dwarf Glass Fish)Gymnochanda ploegi is a tiny, scaleless glass perch from the Sanggau and Kapuas Lakes areas of West Kalimantan, Borneo. It was described in 2014 by Tan and Lim and named for Alex Ploeg, Secretary General of Ornamental Fish International, who died on flight MH17 that year. It lives in still pools near swamp forest and river systems, in soft, acidic water with dense vegetation and low light.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Small, transparent, scaleless body; the swim bladder and spine are visible. Fins: Mature males have expanded second dorsal and anal fins with elongated rays. Females have no fin extensions. Coloration: Males are pinkish with red on the upper half, maroon-red dorsal and anal fins edged in black, and a red-tinted tail. Females are yellowish with reddish tints and mostly clear. Habitat: Still pools near peat swamp forest. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful. Keep in groups of 6 or more. Only house with very small, peaceful soft-water species such as Boraras, tiny tetras, kuhli loaches and small shrimp. Avoid fast or aggressive fish and livebearers.Max Size: About 1.1 in standard lengthTank: Gentle flow, soft light and floating plants for cover.TDS: 20 to 90 ppmpH: 5.0 to 6.5. Absolutely no salt.Temperature: 75 to 82°F; keep stable and well oxygenatedDiet: Micro-predator. Microworms, daphnia, newly hatched brine shrimp and small insects. Once settled, may take finely chopped frozen foods or micro granules.Health: Very delicate and intolerant of poor water quality or high mineral content.Breeding: Captive breeding is difficult due to the tiny fry and specialized spawning behavior.
