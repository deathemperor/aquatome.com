The name is Latin for "lit up", for the bright colors of adult males. It is a stunning nano fish and a captivating addition to planted aquariums thanks to its vibrant color and peaceful nature. Males have red-orange bodies with a neon-blue stripe along the top and electric blue accents on the fins. Females are a more subdued silver with a hint of blue.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Tiny, slim, compressed body with a large eye. Fins: Adult males grow filaments on the second dorsal spine and pelvic fins; median fins have scattered black spots. Coloration: Males are yellow to red-orange with a neon-blue upper stripe. Females are silvery with a hint of blue. Habitat: Densely vegetated swamps and creeks. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful and social. Keep in groups of 6 or more.
