It comes from small blackwater tributaries of the upper Rio Negro near São Gabriel da Cachoeira, Brazil. This cory has a black line across its back and a nice black mask running vertically through the eye area. They are very peaceful and suitable for many community tanks. Just don't keep them with big fish that can try to eat them; it will likely result in the death of both fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Stocky, short-snouted armored catfish with an arched back. Fins: Clear to white fins with sharp spines on the dorsal and pectoral fins. Coloration: Cream to white body with an orange patch on the head, a black bar through the eye and a black stripe along the back. Habitat: Small blackwater streams over sand. Care GuideTemperament: Very peaceful. They are best kept in groups of 5 or more, as they like to school here and there. Small midwater fish encourage them to come out.Max Size: About 2.2 inTank: Soft sand to protect the barbels, shaded areas with wood and plants.pH: Currently kept at a pH of 6 to 7, but they can adapt to a variety of pH (wild range 4.0 to 7.0).Temperature: 72 to 79°FDiet: Currently feeding on blackworms, frozen bloodworms and pellets.Breeding: Spawns in the classic corydoras T-position.
