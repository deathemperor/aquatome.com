It is one of the smallest tetras in the hobby, with a translucent orange to red body. Best kept in a school of 6 or more with plenty of vegetation. Because of their speed, they can be kept with some medium-size mouthbrooders such as Betta taeniata, Betta dimidiata and Betta rubra, as well as other small rasboras and tetras.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Tiny, slender tetra.
