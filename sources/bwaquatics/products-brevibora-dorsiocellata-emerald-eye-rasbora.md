It lives in acidic blackwater streams, rivers and peat swamp lakes of southern Thailand, Peninsular Malaysia and Sumatra. A small schooling nano fish that is perfect for adding life and color to a blackwater biotope. It prefers soft, acidic water and can adapt to both fast-flowing and slow-flowing water.
