The Clown Killifish, also called the Banded or Rocket Panchax, lives in coastal swamps, sluggish streams and small rivers from southern Guinea and Sierra Leone to northwestern Liberia around Monrovia. It stays among marginal vegetation and aquatic plants, and can tolerate slightly brackish water in some places. The water is typically warm, soft and acidic. Deforestation has hit many habitats, so many surviving populations are in protected forest reserves. Unlike many killifish, it is not seasonal and lives in loose groups at the very top of the water.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Tiny, slender surface fish with a flat head and upturned mouth. Fins: Males have a flame-shaped tail with red, yellow and blue bands and extended ventral fins. Females have a rounder, plainer tail. Coloration: Cream to tan-yellow body with four broad black bands. Blue eyes. Habitat: Swamps and shallow, slow forest streams with dense edge plants. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful and timid. Keep in groups of 6 or more. Males display but rarely fight.
