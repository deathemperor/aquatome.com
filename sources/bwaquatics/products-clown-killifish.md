Its striking appearance and relatively easy care requirements make it a popular choice for aquarium hobbyists. They have tan-yellow body with black band and red strips downs he caudal fins. Females have shorter and less colorful fins. Epiplatys annulatus thrives in lowland swamps, sluggish streams, and small rivers within regions characterized by open savannahs and tropical rainforests. It is commonly found in areas with marginal vegetation or aquatic plants. While most habitats are freshwater, it can also tolerate slightly brackish conditions in certain locations.
