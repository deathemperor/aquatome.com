Pseudomugil tenellus lives in warm, vegetated lagoons, swamps and slow streams of northern Australia (Northern Territory and Cape York) and southern Papua New Guinea. These are a beautiful schooling rainbowfish, yellowish orange in color with a metallic streak across the top. Males flick their enlarged dorsal and anal fins at each other constantly. These fish are young adults (approximately 1 in).Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Small, slim body with a large eye. Fins: Two dorsal fins.
