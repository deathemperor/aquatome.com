It has been collected from irrigation ditches, rice paddies and slow streams. As the name suggests, it is a dark, gourami-like fish with a beautiful black outline on its scales. Unlike bettas, these fish tolerate their peers and usually establish a pecking order in groups. They are also very hardy and tolerant of different water temperatures, which makes them great beginner fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Elongated, compressed labyrinth fish. Fins: Males have extended dorsal, anal and tail fins; the tail is rounded. Females have a much rounder body and shorter fins. Coloration: Dark gray to black with black-edged scales. Unlike the similar M. erythropterus, it has no red in the dorsal, anal or tail fins. Habitat: Ditches, rice paddies and slow, vegetated streams.
