Like the name suggest, the Black paradise fish is a dark colored gourami like fish with beautiful black outline on the fishes scales. Males have extended anal dorsal and caudal fins while females have a much rounder body. Unlike bettas, these fish tolerate their peers and usually establish a pecking order in groups. A pair can be kept together in a 10 to 20-gallon tank. They are also very hardy and tolerant of different water temperatures making them great beginner fish.
