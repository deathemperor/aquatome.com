It lives in ponds, rice paddies, ditches and slow streams across southern China, Taiwan, Hainan, Vietnam and Laos, where water temperature changes with the seasons. It is a colorful, gourami-like fish with striking red and blue-green stripes on its flanks. Unlike bettas, these fish tolerate their peers and usually establish a pecking order in groups. They are very hardy and tolerant of different temperatures, which makes them great beginner fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Elongated, compressed labyrinth fish. Fins: Males have long, pointed dorsal and anal fins and a long forked tail. Females have a much rounder body and shorter fins. Coloration: Alternating red-orange and blue-green vertical bars, a dark spot on the gill cover and red tail. Blue, black and albino forms exist in the hobby. Habitat: Still and slow waters: ponds, paddies and ditches. Care GuideTemperament: Somewhat territorial, but can be kept in groups as long as there is enough space. A pair can be kept together in a 5 to 10 gallon tank. Males may fight with other males and with similar-sized fish.Max Size: About 2.5 in body length; up to 4 in including tailTDS: About 60+ ppm (can tolerate high TDS water but prefers low TDS)pH: About 6.0 to 7.8 (prefers softer water)Temperature: 70 to 83°F. Does well in cooler water, but higher temperatures bring out color and breeding behavior. Constant high temperatures can shorten its life.Diet: Carnivorous, but readily takes dried foods.
