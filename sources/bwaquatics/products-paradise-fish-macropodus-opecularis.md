IUCN Red List: LC A colorful Gourami like fish with striking red and green stripes on its flank. They are very wide spread and are widely distributed throughout asia. Males have extended anal dorsal and caudal fins while females have a much rounder body. Unlike bettas, these fish tolerate their peers and usually establish a pecking order in groups.
