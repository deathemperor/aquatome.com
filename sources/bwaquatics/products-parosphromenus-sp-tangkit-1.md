"Tangkit"Parosphromenus sp. "Tangkit" is a little-known, undescribed bintan-type licorice gourami from the Tangkit area of Jambi province, Sumatra. It is traded under the name of its collection area until it can be compared with the described species.Learn more about the genus from our friends at the Parosphromenus Project.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Small and slender. Fins: Dark brown-red unpaired fins with bright blue to white iridescent edges. Coloration: Brown body. Males are more colorful and have longer fins than females. Background: Undescribed form; keep it separate from other Parosphromenus so the locality stays pure. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful and shy. Keep a pair in 5 gal, or a group of 3 to 6 in 10 gal with plenty of caves and leaf litter. They can share a tank with tiny, calm blackwater fish such as Boraras, or with small mouthbrooding bettas like albimarginata and channoides. Never mix different Parosphromenus species or forms.Max Size: About 1.4 inches (estimated from related bintan-type forms)TDS: 0 to 50pH: 4.0 to 6.0Temperature: 72 to 82°FDiet: Micropredator. Wild-caught fish usually take only live food. We currently feed live baby brine shrimp, daphnia and blackworms.Breeding: Expected to follow the bintan type: a cave spawner with a head-down courtship, where the male guards the eggs and larvae until the fry swim free.
