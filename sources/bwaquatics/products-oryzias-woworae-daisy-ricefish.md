It is a beautiful fish for adding color to a small aquarium, with a blue metallic shine in the light and red accents on the edges of the body and fins. Males typically have a translucent bluish body, while females and juveniles are more yellow. They are schooling fish and known jumpers, so a secure lid is a must.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Small, slender body with a flat back and upturned mouth.
