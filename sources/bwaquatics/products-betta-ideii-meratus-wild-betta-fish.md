Betta Idieii are a mouth brooding betta in the Unimaculata complex. They are closely related to Patoti and the two can be difficult to tell apart. Males have unique iridescence and scaling through out their bodies including the typical blue coloration on their cheeks and lower jaws. In Ideii, this iridescence is much more spread and the scaling appears to be more numerous. Ideii also has presence of an orange patch in front of the eye running continuous with upper lip as well as a white vertical patch that is noticeable on the lower lip.
