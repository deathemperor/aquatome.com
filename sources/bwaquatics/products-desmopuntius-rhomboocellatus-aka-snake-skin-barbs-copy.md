Most fish sold as the "Five-Banded Barb" (D. pentazona) are actually this species. It has six dark vertical bands, counting the one through the eye and the one at the tail base. It is calmer than most barbs and does well in blackwater community tanks.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Compact, moderately deep barb. Fins: Red-tinted dorsal and tail fins, brightest in males.
