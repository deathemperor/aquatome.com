It was described in 1991 from a tributary of the Rio Madeira near Ariquemes, Rondônia, Brazil. The name means "similar", for its likeness to C. ourastigma. They are very peaceful and suitable for many community tanks. Just don't keep them with big fish that can try to eat them; it will likely result in the death of both fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Short-snouted, stocky armored catfish. Fins: Clear fins; spotted dorsal fin. Coloration: Pale tan to olive body with small dark spots and a bluish to violet smudge on the rear of the body and tail base. Habitat: Streams of the Rio Madeira basin over sand. Care GuideTemperament: Very peaceful.
