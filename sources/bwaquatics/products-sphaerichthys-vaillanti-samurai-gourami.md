It lives in small, shaded forest creeks full of wood debris, where it hides by looking and moving like a dead leaf. These fish are generally shy and introverted, especially when you first receive them. In this species the females are the more striking ones when they color up, with alternating red and green vertical bars. Unlike the chocolate gourami (S. osphromenoides), the male is the one that holds the eggs in his mouth.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Compact, deep, leaf-shaped body with a pointed head. Fins: Rounded fins; males have longer, more pointed dorsal and anal fins. Coloration: Males are brown to gray-green with faint bars.
