The name means "little tear", for the teardrop-shaped black spot at the base of the tail. It lives in shallow, clear forest streams under canopy. These are fast, agile fish that move in tight schools.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Slender, streamlined rasbora. Fins: Forked tail with a black teardrop spot at its base. Coloration: Silvery body with a cherry-red band along the flanks. Habitat: Shallow, clear streams in lowland forest. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful. Keep in a school of at least 8 to 10.
