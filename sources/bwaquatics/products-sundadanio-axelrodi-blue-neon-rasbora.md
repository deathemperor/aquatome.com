It was named for Herbert Axelrod, who found it in the tanks of a Singapore exporter. It lives in blackwater pools and streams in ancient peat swamp forests of Borneo, Bangka and Sumatra, Indonesia, in dim, acidic water stained brown with tannins from decaying leaves.
