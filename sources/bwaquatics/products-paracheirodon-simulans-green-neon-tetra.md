It lives in shaded, slow blackwater tributaries of the upper Rio Negro and Orinoco basins in Brazil, Colombia and Venezuela, full of tannins and leaf litter. Its turquoise-green stripe runs the full length of the body, and the red on the lower body is limited to the rear half.
