It lives in mangroves, tidal creeks and the lower freshwater reaches of rivers, moving between fresh, brackish and marine water. These pipefish are generally peaceful and stay in the lower zone of a densely planted tank. They are very slow-moving fish.Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Long, thin, armored body made of bony rings, with a tube-shaped snout. Long and skinny rather than bulky. Fins: Small dorsal fin used for slow, upright movement; tiny tail fin. Coloration: Brown to reddish above with fine mottling and pale spots, bluish below with dark bars across the belly. Some dark adults lose the bars. Habitat: Mangroves, tidal creeks and lower river reaches. Care GuideTemperament: Very peaceful. Keep in a species-only tank or with other calm, non-aggressive tankmates that will not compete for food.Max Size: Up to about 7 in (18 cm), but very slimTDS: 300+ ppm; a salt addition is useful. In our experience they have done well both with a little salt and in fully fresh water.pH: 7.0 to 8.4Temperature: 72 to 82°FDiet: Carnivore that needs live or frozen food. Keep flow low or they will have a hard time catching food.Breeding: Males carry the eggs in a brood pouch under the tail and release live young.
