Pseudomugil signifer lives along the east coast of Australia, from Cape York to New South Wales, in streams, lagoons, mangroves and estuaries, in both fresh and brackish water. At least 15 regional forms have been recognized, and size and color vary a lot between them. These are a beautiful schooling rainbowfish, yellowish orange in color with a nice white accent on the fins and bright blue eyes. Their colors are best when breeding. These fish are young adults (approximately 1 in).Physical Characteristics Body Shape: Slim body with a large eye. Fins: Males have enlarged dorsal and anal fins with white and black edges. Coloration: Yellowish orange body with bright blue eyes. Males turn more yellow-gold when breeding. Habitat: Coastal streams, lagoons and estuaries, fresh to brackish. Care GuideTemperament: Peaceful. Pseudomugil species can hybridize, so you may want to keep them with other types of small rainbows, or tetras and rasboras of similar size. They also do well with Neocaridina and Caridina shrimps.Max Size: Usually 1.2 to 1.6 in; males of some populations grow to 2 in or morepH: Currently kept at roughly 6.5 to 7.0Temperature: Currently kept at around 72 to 77°FDiet: Fine flakes, crushed pellets, baby brine shrimp, daphnia and other small live foods.Breeding: Continuous spawner.
