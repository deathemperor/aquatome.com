# Clown Loach Information Posters
by Martin Thoene — last modified Dec 27, 2006 02:10 AM
In an attempt to better educate potential Clown Loach keepers to the space requirements as the fish grow, the poster below has been produced. If you want to help spread the awareness of this species' potential size and need for others of its own kind, you can download the poster in a pdf form and print it out. Ask if your local fish shops are prepared to display it and make a conscious effort not to sell single Clowns or sell to customers with unsuitably sized aquariums.
The movement amongst dedicated hobbyists to bring about a change to more accurate information being displayed and available in aquarium shops is growing. You can help with that process.
The most popular of Loach species all too often gets sold to people who have little idea of the fish's true requirements. A poster cannot give all the required information, but it can raise awareness of the two primary requirements of these beautiful fish. Hopefully, this will lead to a potential owner seeking further information.
Inaccurate information is a world-wide problem within the retail ornamental fish industry. Species totally unsuitable for aquarium keeping are sold on a regular basis. So that this one species may be hopefully given the conditions it requires we will be making available various language versions of the poster. These will be added in due course.
