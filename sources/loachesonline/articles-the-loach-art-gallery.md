# The Loach Art Gallery
by Martin Thoene — last modified Jan 02, 2007 01:08 AM
Over the years, various contributors to Loaches Online have shared their photographic skills with other members. This is the main reason that Loaches Online is the greatest collection of excellent images of the greatest number of Loach species to be found on the Internet.
Many members also possess artistic talent and have been kind enough to share that talent with others. This is a collection of pictures of drawings, paintings, sculpture and other forms of artistic expression featuring our favourite fish.
We are always looking for further art for inclusion in this gallery. If you have artistic abilities, why not submit whatever form of art you specialize in? We would all love to see people's expressions of their love of this hobby, whatever the media used.
Martin Thoene.
