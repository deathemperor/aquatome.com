Relevant Information:
This species (and one other) was removed from Otocinclus into a new genus, Macrotocinclus, by Schaffer (1997) and some publications use this change but it is not widespread at present (2011). The recent paper (2010) by Lehmann, P., F. Mayer and R.E. Reis have resurrected Otocinclus arnoldi from the La Plata basin from the synonymy of O. flexilis and is thus a species in its own right based on three distinguishing features: the possession of five branched pectoral-fin rays, the larger number of enlarged odontodes on the tip of the parieto-supraoccipital posterior process, and having the prootic involved in the contact with the hyomandibular articular condyle.. Aquarium Care: Requires cool waters in the aquarium as sustained high values can be problematic for this species. A planted tank is best as it is kind to plants but will keep the algae at bay. Can be a bit territorial with other fish but do well together in groups. Diet: Algae eater, tablet foods, lettuce, flake and small live and frozen foods. Etymology: The specific name flexilis: Latin for pliant or flexible, allusion not explained nor evident.

IUCN Red List Otocinclus flexilis is endemic to the Patos Lagoon basin, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. The main threat is the loss of habitat quality, mainly in the tributary streams of the Jacui River basin. Even so, it is frequent and abundant. As there is no measurable evidence of threats to the species, it has been categorised as Least Concern (LC), (IUCN 2022). Reference: Baensch, H.A. and R. Riehl 1985 Aquarien atlas. Band 2.
