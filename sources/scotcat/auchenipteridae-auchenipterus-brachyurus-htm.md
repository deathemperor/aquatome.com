Relevant Information:
The genus Auchenipterus is closely related to and is similar to, Epapterus and Pseudepapterus. Distinguished from all species except A. nigripinnis by the presence of a broad, black band that obliquely crosses the middle of the upper lobe of the caudal fin. Aquarium Care: Good community catfish with normal sized patrons but not to be trusted with small Tetras for instance, which will be picked of at night on its twilight patrols. Diet: Can be fed most aquarium fare such as good quality flake, white worm, tablet and pellet foods and frozen foods such as bloodworm. Better to feed at lights out until they get accustomed to the daytime feeding regime when they may very well join in. Etymology: The specific name brachyurus: Short-tailed, from brachýs, short, and urus, from ourá, tail, referring to shorter “caudal region” compared with A. nuchalis. Remarks: This species will not be seen too often in the hobby.
