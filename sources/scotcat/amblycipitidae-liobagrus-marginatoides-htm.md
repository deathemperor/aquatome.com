Relevant Information:
The primary difference between Leiobagrus marginatus & Liobagrus marginatoides is posterior of pectoral spine serrated in L. marginatos. Etymology: The specific name marginatoides: –oides, having the form of: Liobagrus marginatus, to which it is compared.
