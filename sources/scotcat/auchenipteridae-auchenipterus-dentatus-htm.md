Relevant Information:
The genus Auchenipterus is closely related to and is similar to, Epapterus and Pseudepapterus. Habitat: Appears to inhabit the more upstream parts of rivers where it occupies the upper part of the water column. Reproduction: First sexual maturity occurs at a length of 16cm. Reproduction begins at the start of the rainy season. Fertilisation is internal, the female being capable of keeping the sperm in the epithelium of its genital tract. Fecundity is approximately 15,000 eggs. Diet: Active at dusk, feeding mainly on insects and small crustaceans. Etymology: The specific name dentatus: With teeth; serrated. Latin for toothed, referring to velvety teeth on both jaws.

IUCN Red List This species is relatively widespread across Suriname and French Guiana with an extent of occurrence (EOO) approaching 100,000 km2. Rivers in French Guiana and Suriname have been suffering impacts from small-scale gold mining. Further research into the population status is recommended. However, given the widespread distribution, this species is assessed as Least Concern (IUCN 2022). Reference:
