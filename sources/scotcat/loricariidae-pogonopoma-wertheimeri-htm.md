Relevant Information:
Habitat: Currently known from three coastal river drainages in southeastern Brazil. Usually found in stretches of river with mid to strong water current and rocky or sandy bottom; although some specimens were collected from a lentic habitat. Etymology: The specific name wertheimeri: Patronym not identified, in honour of either Louis Wertheimer of the Thayer Expedition to Brazil (1865–1866), or Achilles Wertheimer, who died on the expedition due to a snakebite.

IUCN Red List Pogonopoma wertheimeri occurs in the Mucuri and São Mateus river basins, having been translocated to the Doce and Barra Seca river basins. The species is widely distributed, frequent, and abundant. Despite the threats associated with the Santa Clara hydroelectric dam and river siltation, these were not considered significant for the population. Therefore, Pogonopoma wertheimeri was categorised as Least Concern (LC), (IUCN 2022). Reference:
