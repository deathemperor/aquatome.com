Tachysurus trilineatus (Zheng, 1979)

Relevant Information:
Description: Dorsal spines (total): 2; Dorsal soft rays (total): 7; Anal soft rays: 18 - 19. Nude without scale, complete lateral line. Well developed dorsal fin, adipose fin about 1.4-2.8 times the length of the dorsal fin, nearly arc-shaped. Colouration: Yellowish brown in colour, with three dark yellowish lateral strips in the side (trilineatus). One of the strips along the lateral line is discontinued. Etymology: The genus name of Tachysurus: tachys, swift; oura, tail, referring to the presumed agility of T. sinensis based on its “long and nimble” tail (translation). The specific name of trilineatus: ri-, three; lineatus, lined, referring to three yellowish lines on each side of body. Remarks: Still valid in some publications as Pseudobagrus trilineatus but Eschmeyer's Catalog of Fishes has Tachysurus as the current genus name with Pseudobagrus trilineatus as a junior synonym.
