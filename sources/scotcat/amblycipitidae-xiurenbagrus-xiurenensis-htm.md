Relevant Information:
Description: There are three species in this genera: X. dorsalis, X. xiurenensis and X. gigas. Dorsal spines (total): 1; Dorsal soft rays (total): 6 - 6; Anal soft rays: 7 - 8; Vertebrae: 41 - 44. Elongated body, round-shaped in the front, gradually flat in the back, covered by membrane. Short dorsal fin; very small eyes, covered by skin membrane. Dorsal-fin rays i,6; Anal-fin rays vi,8 to vi,11; Pectoral-fin rays i,6 to i,7; Pelvic-fin rays i,6. Colouration: The edge of dorsal, anal and caudal fins are yellowish; brownish back, yellowish stomach. Etymology: The specific name xiurenensis: –ensis, suffix denoting place: Xiuren River, Pearl River drainage, Guangxi Province, China, type locality.
