Relevant Information:
Description: D I, 7; A 14-17; P1 I, 11-12; P2 6; GR 4-5 + 9-10. Body elongated, compressed. Head depressed; head shield almost smooth, weakly striated; dorsomedian groove shallow and short, not reaching anteriorly to level of posterior end of eye; supraoccipital process rounded in adult. Palatal teeth granular or conical with blunt tips, on 2 pairs of patches longitudinally arranged; anterior patch small and oval; posterior patch elongate, slender. Inner gill rakes absent on the anterior 2 gill arches. Lateral line bifurcates behind caudal fin base. Colouration: Body silvery; brownish dorsally; all fins blackish or dusky; adipose fin with a black margin. Habitat: Distributed in eastern Indian Ocean and West Pacific, from east coast of India to east coast of Australia, north to Philippines. Inhabits coastal waters and estuaries. Diet: Feeds mainly on sea urchins. Etymology: The genus name Plicofollis: plico, fold; follis, bellows or windbag, referring to creased or scalloped swim bladder characteristic of the genus. The specific name nella: derived from nalla-jella, local name for this catfish in Vizagapatam, India.
