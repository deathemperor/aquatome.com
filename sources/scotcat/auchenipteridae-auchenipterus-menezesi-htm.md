Relevant Information:
The genus Auchenipterus is closely related to and is similar to, Epapterus and Pseudepapterus. Aquarium Care: Good community catfish with normal sized patrons but not to be trusted with small Tetras for instance, which will be picked of at night on its twilight patrols. Diet: In its natural habitat, microcrustaceans, algae, and insects were among the food found routinely in the gut contents. Can be fed most aquarium fare such as good quality flake, white worm, tablet and pellet foods and frozen foods such as bloodworm. Better to feed at lights out until they get accustomed to the daytime feeding regime when they may very well join in. Etymology: The specific name, menezesi, is in honour of Dr. Rui Simões de Menezes, Universidade Federal do Ceará, Brazil, in recognition to his studies of the life history of freshwater fishes of northeastern Brazil, including this species.

IUCN Red List Auchenipterus menezesi occurs in the Parnaíba and Pindaré-Mirim river basins, in the states of Maranhão, Ceará, and Piauí. The species is frequent, although not very abundant. No significant threats to its population have been identified. For these reasons, A. menezesi has been categorised as Least Concern (IUCN 2022). Reference:
