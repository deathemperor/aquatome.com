Relevant Information:
Habitat: From the Paraguay River at Coromba, Brazil. Aquarium Care: Peaceful species for a mid sized community tank, planted with hiding places such as pipes or driftwood/bogwood. Diet: Tablet and pellet foods, frozen bloodworm and live foods should keep this catfish in good condition. Etymology: The specific name paraguayensis: –ensis, Latin suffix denoting place: Corumba, Paraguay, type locality.
