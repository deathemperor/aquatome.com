Relevant Information:
This species can be variable in colouration and is also quite rare in the hobby. Lineage: Placed in Lineage 1, and when there is a revision they will stay as Corydoras (Lacépède, 1803) with the type species of C. geoffroy La Cépède, 1803. As of the latest update (Dias et al 2024), Corydoras serratus has been placed in Lineage 1 and has retained the genus name of Corydoras. Aquarium Care: As this is one of the longer snouted species it will be well suited to most community tanks. A well planted tank with a soft substrate such as sand so they can bury their snouts while searching out food. Diet: Readily accepts a mixed and varied diet which includes granular foods, tablet food, frozen bloodworm and a good quality flake. Etymology: The specific name serratus: Latin for toothed like a saw, referring to “distinctively serrated or toothed” pectoral-fin spines. Remarks: C146, C038 and CW075 are the same species as C. serratus.

IUCN Red List Corydoras serratus is endemic to Brazil and known only from the holotype, collected in the upper Rio Negro basin, in the state of Amazonas. Although collection for commercial purposes is authorized, this activity does not appear to harm the species' survival in the wild. Although population information is not available, the region is in good conservation status and no significant threats have been identified. Therefore, C. serratus has been categorised as Least Concern (IUCN 2022). Reference:
