Relevant Information:
Habitat: Freshwater; brackish; benthopelagic; amphidromous. Found in freshwaters and tidal rivers. Etymology: The specific name burmanicus: Burmese, referring to type locality at Salween, Burma (now Myanmar; also occurs in India and Bangladesh). Remarks: Still found on some online searches listed as Cochlefelis burmanicus.

- IUCN Red List Cochlefelis burmanicus is known from the lower reaches and estuaries of the Irrawaddy and Salween drainages eastwards to the western part of the Malay Peninsula. Whilst there is a lack of information on the species' range, distribution, and threats, it is assessed as Least Concern given its presence in a number of river basins and the absence of known threats. The species should be reassessed if further information becomes available on the likely or actual impact of hydropower dams on the Irrawaddy and Salween rivers (IUCN 2010). Reference: Ferraris, C.J.
