Relevant Information:
Habitat: Ernstichthys live among the cobble substrates of piedmont rivers gushing out of the Andes Mountains, from Venezuela to southern Peru. (Lujan, N). Aquarium Care: Provide fine sand for them, which they use to burry themselves. Etymology: The genus name of Ernstichthys: m eans “Fish of Ernst”; A. Ernst worked at the Universidade Central de Venezuela. The specific name megistus: probably latinised form of the ancient Greek word megistos, which means “the greatest”.

IUCN Red List Although there are few records of the species in the literature and ichthyological collections, Ernstichthys megistus seems to be potentially present in a large area, since these few records are from the Río Pastaza and Río Marañon basins in Ecuador and in the upper Río Madre de Dios and in the Apurimac River Valley in Peru. There is no information of its population, habitat and ecology, but also there is no evident threat affecting the species. Thus, Ernstichthys megistus is assessed as Least Concern (LC), (IUCN 2023).
