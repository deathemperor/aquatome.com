Relevant Information:
Habitat: Mainly marine but frequently found in brackish estuaries, and sometimes enters freshwater. Common during winter. Ornamental and sometimes considered an aquarium fish, the fish is venomous and can be dangerous to humans. Diet: Feeds on fish, benthic invertebrates, zooplankton and detritus. Etymology: The specific name latiscutatus: latis, broad; scutatus, shielded, referring to occipital process broader than long. Remarks: It has to be noted that Marceniuk et al (2007) have placed this species (Arius latiscutatus) in a new Genera, Carlarius, named for Carl Ferraris for his contribution to the knowledge of Siluriformes fishes and includes the seven known Arius species from the west African coastline. Arius heudeloti Valenciennes, 1840, is the type species. Up to date this change of genera has now been accepted.

6.5-8.0. IUCN Red List This species can be found from Mauritania to Namibia, is mainly found in estuaries and can be locally abundant. It is fished throughout its range in artisanal and industrial fisheries. Species-specific catch data are not available, but mixed-Ariidae catch reported in the region do not show any current indication of decline. It is listed as Least Concern. However, this species should be carefully monitored, as it may be increasingly targeted as other demersal species are over-exploited (IUCN 2020).
