Relevant Information:
Most similar to G. melanopterus in colouration and having the highest gill raker counts among its congeners. Description: Dorsal spines (total): 1 - 1; Dorsal soft rays (total): 6 - 6; Vertebrae: 38 – 39. Head and body silvery, without pattern. Fins, except caudal fin, black distally, with clear basal portions; caudal fin entirely clear. Dorsal spine extending well past adipose-fin origin when adpressed. Anal fin with 5-6 simple rays, 11 branched rays. Premaxilla with 4 rows of teeth. Etymology: The specific name gagata: Local Bengali name for this catfish in India.
