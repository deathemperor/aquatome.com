Relevant Information:
This genus was previously placed in its own family of Ageneiosidae and you can still find some publications that still follow this line. Habitat: Occurs in the quiet zones of swamps or the lower parts of stream. Aquarium Care: As per this genera they will be fine as juveniles, but as they mature they become predatory so would need to be kept with fish that are not too small. A large tank would be needed with plenty of swimming space. Diet: Carnivorous. Swims close to the bottom, feeding mainly on fish. Etymology: The genus name Ageneiosus: beardless, pertaining to the very small maxillary barbels. The specifc epithet ucayalensis: named for the Ucayali River, Peru, South America.
