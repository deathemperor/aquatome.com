Relevant Information:
Can walk and leave the water to migrate to other water bodies using its auxiliary breathing organs. Description: Dorsal spines (total): 0. Anterior edge of pectoral spine strongly serrated; head length (from tip of snout to extremity of occipital process) 4.0-4.3 times in SL; blackish body. Habitat: Lives in lowland wetlands, bogs and peats. Found in swamps with slightly acidic water. Occur in standing water habitats. Etymology: The specific name meladerma: mélas (Gr.), black; dérma (Gr.), skin, referring to its blackish or dark body colouration, sometimes variegated with irregular, deeply black spots.
