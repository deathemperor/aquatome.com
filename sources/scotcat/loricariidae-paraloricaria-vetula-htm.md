Relevant Information:
Aquarium Care: Grows rather large and would need a large tank to accommodate it and a good water flow in the aquarium. Diet: In their natural habitat part of their diet is the mollusc, Limnoperna fortunei which is an invasive species. Etymology: According to Valenciennes (1840), Latin cognate of vieille, old woman, from its local common name, vielle à longue queue (“long-tailed old woman”), referring to its filamentous caudal-fin ray; vieille (velha in Portuguese, vieja in Spanish) is a name it shares with several loricariid, doradid and callichthyid catfishes (e.g., Loricariichthys anus) in Buenos Aires, Argentina, allusion not explained nor evident. Remarks: Not exported very often due to their large size.
