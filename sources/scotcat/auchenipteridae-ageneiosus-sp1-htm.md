Relevant Information:
This genus was previously placed in its own family of Ageneiosidae and you can still find some publications that still follow this line. Aquarium Care: Although reputed to be of a smaller size they could still prove to be predatory as per this genera, so would need to be kept with fish that are not too small. Diet: Presumably meat based. Etymology: The genus name Ageneiosus: beardless, pertaining to the very small maxillary barbels.
