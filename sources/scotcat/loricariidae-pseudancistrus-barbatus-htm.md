Relevant Information:
Habitat: Occurs in rapids where it lives in the rocky anfractuosities in the strong current zones. Fished by harpoons or bows in the shallow zones. Aquarium Care: Not the easiest species to maintain in the aquarium as it will need pristine water conditions with well filtered water which should not be too hard. Warm water should be the norm. Adult males are very territorial and will not get on with other large Loricariid catfish. Sexual Differences: The length of the male snout bristles might be related with the hierarchic level of the individual within the population. Males have a broad head and posses long bristles along the edge of the snout. Diet: Aufwuch grazer, mainly vegetarian, with lettuce, spinach, cucumber and courgette (zucchini), vegetarian flake and tablets. Will eat other foods also such as insect larvae, zooplankton and soft wood but will need to be kept on a mainly green diet. Etymology: The specific name barbatus: Bearded, alluding to bristles.

IUCN Red List Pseudancistrus barbatus is restricted to rivers basins in French Guiana, where it occurs in the Marowijne River at the border with Suriname, in Mana River, and in Oyapock River at the border with Brazil. The extent of occurrence (EOO) is estimated at 87,877 km2, and the area of occupancy (AOO) at 40,288 km2. There are local impacts in the lower portion of Oyapock basin in the coastal line of French Guiana close to the populated areas, and also along the main channel of Marowijne River basin along several cities, associated with pollution, increase of sediment, and agriculture activities impacting the ecosystem. Additionally, the species is sought after as part of a small-scale fishery by local communities, and illegal gold mining is fairly widespread in French Guiana. Except for local impacts, the overall distribution region seems relatively well preserved and there are no major threats. Thus, Pseudancistrus barbatus is assessed as Least Concern (LC), (IUCN 2022).
