Relevant Information:
The dorsal and pectoral spines are venomous on this species. Habitat: Found along the coastline, mainly around estuaries. Ascends into fresh water of the upper tidal zone. Occurs in the Mekong delta. Reproduction: They carry the eggs in their mouths. Juveniles continue to hide in males' mouths for the first 2 months. Diet: Feeds on invertebrates and small fishes. Etymology: The genus name Hexanematichthys: Greek, exa = six + Greek, nema = fialement + Greek, ichthys = fish. The species epithet sagor: Local Bengali name for this fish in India.
