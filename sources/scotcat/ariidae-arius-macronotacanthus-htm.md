Relevant Information:
Was described by Pieter Bleeker in 1846, originally under the genus Arius and after two genera and specific name changes has returned to its original description. Habitat: It inhabits southern and southeastern Asian brackish waters, also occurring in marine and freshwaters but predominantly in brackish waters. Etymology: The specific name macronotacanthus: macro-, large; notos, back; acanthus, spine, referring to the thick dorsal spine. Remarks: Can sometimes be found on some online searches as Nemapteryx macronotacantha.
