Relevant Information:
Description: The tip of adpressed dorsal fin comes close to the adipose-fin origin as well as the tip of the pectoral fin is close to the pelvic-fin origin and the tip of pelvic fin reaches the anal-fin origin. It has a distinct serrae on the anterior and especially the posterior pectoral spine margins. It has small spots distributed all over the flank or with spots faint in the posterior portion of body. Noted for their unusual shaped mouth which is long and conical. They also possess large eyes. Three species are described in this genera, I. megalops and I. labrosus. and the latest, Iheringichthys syi Azpelicueta & Britski, 2012. Etymology: The specific name megalops: mégas, big; ops, eye, referring to its very large eyes, larger than those of I. labrosus. Remarks: Not found very often in the hobby as imports from Paraguay are not often obtained.
