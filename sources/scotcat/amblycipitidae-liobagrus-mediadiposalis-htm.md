Relevant Information:
Description: Dorsal spines (total): 1; Dorsal soft rays (total): 6 - 6; Anal spines: 0; Anal soft rays: 14 -17. Habitat: Endemic to Korea and inhabits the rocky or stony bottoms of rocky streams. The streams are well oxygenated but during the summer dry season, they become hypoxic. L. mediadiposalis may be able to absorb oxygen through the skin during this period. Etymology: –alis, adjectival suffix: media-, middle, presumably referring to high and long adipose fin centered on back between dorsal and caudal fins.

6.5-7.5. IUCN Red List This species is observed in central and southern parts of the Korean Peninsula, where it is endemic. Despite evidence to suggest that some habitats along the Geum River have been destroyed, no major threats to the species have been identified so far and there are no reports of population declines. This species is assessed as Least Concern, but it is recommended that threats and population trends be investigated (IUCN 2023). Reference: Ferraris, C.J. Jr., 2007.
