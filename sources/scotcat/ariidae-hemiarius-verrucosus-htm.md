Hemiarius verrucosus (Ng, 2003)
Image contributors to this species:

ScotCat Sources:

Relevant Information:
Habitat: Inhabits lower reaches of rivers and estuaries and feeds on fishes and shellfishes. Reproduction: In most , if not all species, the male carries the relatively large eggs in its mouth until hatching. Etymology: The specific name verrucosus: warty, referring to numerous small bumps on pectoral spines and dorsal surface of neurocranium.
