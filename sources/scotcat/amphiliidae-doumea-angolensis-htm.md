Doumea angolensis Boulenger, 1906

Relevant Information:
Description: Caudal peduncle is very long and slim. The adipose fin begins above the centre of the anal fin, and the caudal fin is moderately sickle-shaped, its lobes roughly the same size. Colouration: Body is yellowish with grey overtones on the back. A number of indistinct darker spots are superimposed on the background. The fins exibit more or less well transverse bands. Habitat: Preferred habitat is not known, but occurs in small rivers with running water. Biology is not known in detail, but species of this genus are adapted to live in rapids. Etymology: The specific name angolensis: meaning from Angola.
