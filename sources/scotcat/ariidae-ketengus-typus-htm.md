Relevant Information:
Description: Teeth absent from roof of mouth with the mouth opening wide, extending far beyond eye. Only one species recorded (monotypic). Habitat: Found in the lower parts of rivers in either fresh or brackish waters. Occurs in coastal waters from Thailand to Indonesia and westward to the Andaman Islands. Diet: Feeds on invertebrates and small fishes. Etymology: The genus name ketengus: latinisation of Keteng, Madurese name for this catfish in eastern Java, Indonesia. The specific name typus: serving as type of genus. Remarks: Caught with trawls, seines, and traps.
