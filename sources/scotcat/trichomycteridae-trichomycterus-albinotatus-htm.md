Relevant Information:
Trichomycterus albinotatus is a common but not very abundant species. Habitat: Trichomycterus albinotatus is endemic to the Paraíba do Sul basin. It is collected with some frequency, but is not very abundant. Etymology: The specific name albinotatus: albus (L.), white; notatus (L.), marked, referring to white marks on upper body.

IUCN Red List Trichomycterus albinotatus is endemic to the Paraíba do Sul basin. It is collected fairly frequently, but is not very abundant. It occurs in an area impacted by urbanisation, which apparently does not represent a significant threat that puts its population at risk, and is categorised as Least Concern (LC), (IUCN 2022).
