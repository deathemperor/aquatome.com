Relevant Information:
Habitat: Trichomycterus mirissumba is endemic to the middle portion of the Paraíba do Sul River basin, in the states of Minas Gerais and Rio de Janeiro. The species is not very abundant and infrequent, found within the limits of the Serra da Mantiqueira APA. Etymology: The specific name mirissumba: Local name for this catfish in the village of Maromba (Estado do Rio de Janeiro, Brazil), probably derived from the Tupí-Guaraní, its meaning unknown (tadpoles).
