Relevant Information:
Description: Distinguished from its congeners by the following characters; a relatively long dorsal spine (7.3-12.4 % SL) and outer mental barbel (15.3-21.6 % SL), and a short distance from dorsal-fin insertion to adipose-fin origin (13.5-22.0 % SL). Habitat: L. somjinensis is restricted to rivers flowing to the west coast and Geogeum Island, the Somjin, Dongin and Tamjin rivers (Kim S-h, et al. 2023). Colouration: A broad yellowish, vertical, crescent-shaped band in the middle of the caudal fin; deep black colouration on the outer margin of the dorsal, anal, and caudal fins. Etymology: –ensis, suffix denoting place: Somjin River, South Korea, the type locality.
