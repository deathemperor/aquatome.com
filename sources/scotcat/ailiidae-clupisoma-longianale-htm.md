Relevant Information:
This is only the third species in the nine Clupisoma genera recorded that is found in China. The other two are Clupisoma nujiangense and Clupisoma sinense. Reproduction: Oviparous, eggs are unguarded with distinct pairing of the adults. Etymology: The genus name Clupisoma: clupea, herring; soma, body, referring to the herring-shaped body. The specific name longianale: longus, long; analis, anal, referring to longer anal fin compared to C. sinense. Remarks: Clupisoma longianale is still listed in Fishbase as being Clupisoma longianalis but it is updated in The Catalog of Fishes as the current name of Clupisoma longianale.
