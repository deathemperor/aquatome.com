Relevant Information:
Description: Palatal tooth patches distinctly triangular, their long axes at about 70° to curve of mouth. Etymology: The genus name Kyataphisa: Bengali word meaning catfish. The specific name nenga: from Nenga gagora, local Bengali name for this catfish in India. Remarks: On some online searches this species can still be found as Nemapteryx nenga.
Common Name:
Thickspined catfish, Nenga gagora
Synonyms:
Pimelodus nenga, Arius nenga, Tachysurus nenga, Tachysurus caelatus, Arius caelatus, Nemapteryx caelata, Nemapteryx nenga
Family:
Ariidae
Distribution:
South and southeast Asia: India, Thailand, Myanmar, Malaysia and Indonesia. Type locality: Ganges River, India.
