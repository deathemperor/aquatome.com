Relevant Information:
The common name of 'False Cory' is due to its habit of schooling with Corydoras species. This habit probably stems from the protection of this scaleless catfish from large predators due to the plated Corydoras where predators are wary of the locked pectoral and dorsal spines. There is the other scenario that states that the Brachyrhamdia are practising a Batesian form of shoaling wheras they take food that the Corydoras dig up from the substrate and also they stael the eggs of these species. They seem to do better with Corydoras that possess a black eye stripe such as Hoplisoma (Corydoras) melanistium rather than plain species such as Osteogaster (Corydoras) aenea which tend to be nipped and driven out of the shoal. Aquarium Care: House in aquaria (24ins/60cm or over) with alternate sand and gravel substrate. Provide planted area. Keep in a species tank or with Corydoras and larger Characins or Anabantoides in the top half as they can be predatory with small species such as Neon Tetras. Diet: Tablet and pellet foods. Frozen and live foods such as bloodworm. Insect larvae. Not a fussy eater. Etymology: Brachy = short; rhamdia = from the vernucular name 'Nhamdia or 'Jamdia. The specific epithet of 'imitator' means to imitate another.
