# Philodendron sp. 'Mini Midget'
### Layouts containing this plant
Philodendron 'Mini Midget' is a compact epiphytic species that brings a lush tropical appearance to any terrarium. Its dense growth and deep green foliage create a rich, natural look while remaining remarkably manageable in size. Only under particularly favourable conditions will it exceed 20 cm in height. This versatile plant can be grown attached to wood or other hardscape materials, where its epiphytic nature is best displayed. It can also be cultivated in pots, provided the roots are not kept excessively wet. Discovered in a greenhouse in Florida in 2016, its true origin remains uncertain, though it is believed to stem from the Ecuadorian rainforest. With its compact growth, attractive foliage, and easy cultivation, Philodendron 'Mini Midget' is a hidden gem for tropical terrariums and paludariums.
