# Macodes sp. 'Silver Mine'
### Layouts containing this plant
Macodes sp. 'Silver Mine' is an eye-catching jewel orchid that adds a touch of elegance to humid tropical terrariums. Its velvety green leaves are adorned with intricate silvery veins that shimmer in the light, making it a natural focal point even without flowers. This slow-growing species thrives in warm, humid environments where it can be grown in airy, well-drained substrates or attached to wood and stone as an epiphyte. The creeping growth habit allows it to gradually establish attractive colonies while remaining easy to manage. To ensure healthy growth, the roots should be kept moist but never waterlogged. With its remarkable leaf pattern and relatively undemanding nature, Macodes sp. 'Silver Mine' is an excellent choice for anyone looking to add a rare and exotic highlight to a tropical terrarium.
