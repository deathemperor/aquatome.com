# Najas guadalupensis 'Guppy Grass'
### Layouts containing this plant
Originating from the USA and South America, Najas guadalupensis is an exceptionally easy aquatic plant that thrives under almost any conditions. It grows quickly and propagates effortlessly, making it ideal for beginners as well as experienced aquarists. Although usually kept as a free-floating plant, it may anchor itself in the substrate with long, delicate roots. The fine, porous structure provides excellent shelter for fish fry and shrimp, while also helping to absorb excess nutrients and produce oxygen. Perfect for both aquariums and ponds, Najas guadalupensis supports a healthy, balanced environment and offers a natural hiding place for aquatic life.
