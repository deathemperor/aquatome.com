# Peperomia prostrata
### Layouts containing this plant
Peperomia prostrata, better known as String of Turtles, is a highly sought-after terrarium plant that is now available in vitro for the first time globally. Its small, round leaves feature a striking pattern reminiscent of miniature turtle shells, combining light green markings with rich brown tones to create a truly unique appearance. This versatile species can be used in many ways within a terrarium. It readily creeps across the substrate as a decorative carpet, climbs humid backgrounds, or cascades elegantly from wood and hardscape features. Its compact growth and distinctive foliage make it an eye-catching addition to both small and large tropical setups. With its unusual leaf pattern and flexible growth habit, String of Turtles adds character, texture, and visual interest to any terrarium design.
