# Sagittaria subulata 'Needle Leaf'
### Layouts containing this plant
Sagittaria sp. 'Needle' is a stunning, narrow-leaved aquatic plant, likely a variation of Sagittaria subulata. With slender leaves reaching 15–30 cm in length and only a few millimeters wide, this plant adds a delicate, elegant touch to any freshwater aquarium. Ideal for aquascaping, Sagittaria 'Needle' is easy to grow, thriving in moderate light without the need for additional CO2. Under intense lighting, its vibrant green foliage may develop subtle orange hues, enhancing its visual appeal. This hardy plant spreads effortlessly via runners, making propagation simple—just trim and replant as needed. Thanks to its compact, upright growth, Sagittaria 'Needle' is a fantastic choice for creating a soft, natural-looking background in aquariums. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced aquascaper, this low-maintenance plant is a great addition to any aquatic setup.
