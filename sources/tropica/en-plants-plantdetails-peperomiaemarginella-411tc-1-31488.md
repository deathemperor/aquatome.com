# Peperomia emarginella
### Layouts containing this plant
Peperomia emarginella is a delicate, small-leaved species from tropical America that adds fine texture and detail to terrarium layouts. Its tiny emerald-green leaves grow densely along slender stems, forming attractive carpets or cascading growth that softens hardscape and creates a natural jungle appearance. Thanks to its versatile growth habit, it can be used as a foreground plant, allowed to trail from branches and rocks, or encouraged to climb moist backgrounds. The distinctive rounded leaves, with their subtly notched shape, give the plant a refined appearance despite its miniature size. With moderate growth and easy propagation from stem cuttings, Peperomia emarginella is an excellent choice for adding intricate detail to tropical terrariums while remaining easy to maintain.
