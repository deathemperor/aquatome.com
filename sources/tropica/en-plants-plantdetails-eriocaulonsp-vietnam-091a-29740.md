# Eriocaulon sp. 'Vietnam'
### Layouts containing this plant
Eriocaulon sp. 'Vietnam' is a striking aquatic plant with thin, spiky, bright green leaves forming a rosette at the base, resembling pine needles. This plant requires medium light levels and benefits from CO2 supplementation for optimal growth. It thrives in nutrient-rich substrates and soft, slightly acidic water. Regular fertilization and maintenance are essential to prevent nutrient deficiencies and promote healthy growth.
