# Streptocarpus saxorum
### Layouts containing this plant
Streptocarpus saxorum, commonly known as the False African Violet, is a charming terrarium plant valued for its delicate pale lilac flowers and compact growth habit. While it typically remains low and bushy, the abundance of blooms adds a striking splash of colour that contrasts beautifully with its soft green foliage. Unlike many tropical terrarium plants, S. saxorum prefers conditions that are not excessively wet. As a succulent species, it thrives in well-drained, breathable substrates and benefits from moderate humidity levels. These characteristics make it an excellent choice for terrariums with good air circulation and slightly drier growing conditions. Its combination of elegant flowers, manageable size, and unique cultivation requirements makes Streptocarpus saxorum a distinctive addition to any terrarium collection.
