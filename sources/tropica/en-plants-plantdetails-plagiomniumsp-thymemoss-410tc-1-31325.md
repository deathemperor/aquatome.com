# Plagiomnium sp. 'Thyme Moss'
### Layouts containing this plant
Plagiomnium sp. 'Thyme Moss' is an attractive moss with a distinctive appearance that sets it apart from many other mosses used in terrariums. Resembling the North American species Plagiomnium cuspidatum, it develops comparatively large leaves that create a lush and textured look. The bright green leaves are translucent and feature elegant wavy margins, giving the moss a fresh, vibrant appearance under humid terrarium conditions. Its larger leaf size adds visual interest and contrast when combined with finer mosses or tropical plants. Easy to incorporate into natural layouts, Plagiomnium sp. 'Thyme Moss' is ideal for creating soft green carpets, covering decorative elements, or bringing a woodland-like atmosphere to terrariums and paludariums.
