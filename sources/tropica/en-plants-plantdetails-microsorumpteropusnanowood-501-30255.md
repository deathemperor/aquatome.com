# Microsorum pteropus nano wood
### Layouts containing this plant
Microsorum pteropus 'Narrow', commonly known as Narrow Leaf Java Fern, is a popular choice among aquarists for its slender, arching leaves and adaptability. When pre-attached to nano wood, it offers a convenient and aesthetically pleasing addition to any aquarium. This combination provides a natural look, with the fern's delicate leaves gracefully extending from the textured wood, creating an immediate focal point.
This plant thrives in low to moderate lighting and does not require CO₂ supplementation, making it suitable for beginners and seasoned hobbyists alike. Its slow growth rate means minimal maintenance, and it can tolerate a wide range of water conditions. The pre-attached nano wood ensures easy placement; simply position it in your desired location without the need for additional anchoring.
Key Benefits
- Ready to Use: Pre-attached to nano wood for effortless integration.
- Low Maintenance: Thrives in various conditions with minimal care.
- Natural Aesthetics: Adds immediate visual appeal with its elegant form.
Microsorum pteropus 'Narrow' on Nano Wood is an excellent choice for aquarists seeking to enhance their aquascape with minimal effort, providing both beauty and functionality.
