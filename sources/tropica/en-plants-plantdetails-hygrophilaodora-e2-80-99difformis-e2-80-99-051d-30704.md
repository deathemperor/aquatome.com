# Hygrophila odora ’Difformis’
### Layouts containing this plant
Hygrophila Odora is a vibrant, fast-growing plant that adds a fresh, bright green touch to any aquarium. Known for its bushy, dense foliage, this plant is perfect for aquascapers looking to quickly fill in midground or background spaces with lush greenery. Its rapid growth rate and adaptability make it ideal for beginners and experts alike, providing a natural, thriving appearance with minimal effort. This resilient plant is undemanding, thriving in a variety of water conditions and lighting levels, although moderate lighting will yield the best results. With regular trimming, Hygrophila Odora maintains a compact, bushy shape, making it a versatile addition for any aquascape style.
