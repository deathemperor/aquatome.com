# Long Tranhoang | Diorama
August 23, 2019 1 min read
This tank makes sophisticated usage of chiseled rock edges, angled to give dramatic shadows beneath each rock. Floating pieces of rock create a sense of wonder. However, other details are handled well to prevent the aquacape from feeling overly unnatural. Thus, mosses and epiphytes are used to soften the edges and connections between rock pieces while fine wood details give an aged look to the aquascape.
