# Dou Mok | Nature Style
March 09, 2022 1 min read
This charming aquascape by Dou Mok uses humble round rocks as the bedrock of the scape. Their round textures and naturalistic coloration offers good contrast to the fine textures of the plants used and is reminiscent of stones found in fast flowing river. Plant choice is carefully orchestrated here, providing a good variety of colors and textures without being as stark contrast as Dutch scapes - which lends a naturalistic feel to the aquascape.
