# Hiep Hong | Hardscape Focused
March 13, 2024 1 min read
This aquascape shows what a great impact a good hardscape arrangement can have in a tank even if it was not planted. The high mounting position of the light unit gives good air space allows hardscape to be worked beyond the tank surface. Notice how all the wood are arranged to originate from a common point which mimics the natural growth branch arrangement of many trees. This theme of mimicking nature is something done in most aquascapes to give a sense of naturalness to aquascapes.
