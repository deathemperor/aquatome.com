May 21, 2020 1 min read
This immense and meticulously maintained 'Dutch Style' inspired tank highlights the vibrance of individual species through a vast selection of contrasting shapes and colours. Short carpets and shrubs in the foreground evolve to mid-height, then tall stem plants in the background, creating a sense of perspective in the overall aquascape.
