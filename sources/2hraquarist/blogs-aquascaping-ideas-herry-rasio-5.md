# Herry Rasio 5 | Diorama / Nature Style
June 19, 2024 1 min read
This aquascape by Herry rasio employs strong repeated diagonal lines to inject a sense of dynamism and movement to the hardscape work. The 2 key lessons here: repeated patterns reinforce an impression and diagonal composition lines gives a lot of movement to an aquascape. The downside of having too strict a repeated pattern is that the composition might appear a tad contrived. This is balanced by the naturalistic planting style; which utilizes a good mixture of different species to give variety.
