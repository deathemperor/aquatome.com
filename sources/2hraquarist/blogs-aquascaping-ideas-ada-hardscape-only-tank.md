# ADG | Hardscape Focused
October 04, 2019 1 min read
This aquascape by US based ADG (Aquarium Design Group) shows the potential of a tank that does not have any plants at all. The setup works because of the carefully chosen tones of the sand, stones and drift wood- which complement each other and appear as a single unit, albeit of different materials.
