# Andre Longarco 1 | Diorama
October 11, 2019 1 min read
Rock ridges align with the rows of stem plants to evoke a strong sense of flow that pulls the viewer into the scene. Lush and colorful stem plants adds a sense of vibrancy to the aquascape. A lighted back ground furthers the sense of depth and space beyond the confines of the tank. The fish are well positioned to look like they are in the distance which adds to the sense of scale in the aquascape.
