# Wolinski Gregoire | Iwagumi / Diorama
February 26, 2020 1 min read
This rock scape by Wolinski gregoire features imposing rock pillars. The tank size measures 120 x 55 x 47cm and the illusion of depth comes from constructing smaller pillars and rock formations towards the rear of the tank that share similar characteristics with the larger pillars at the front; the contrast that the viewer sees when comparing the two layers generate the illusion of the rear structures being much further away. Indistinct mosses blend and hide seams of hardscape construction and not give away the scale of structures in the tank.
