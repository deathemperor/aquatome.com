# Indra bob wijaya | Diorama
May 15, 2024 1 min read
This impressive aquascape by Indra Bob Wijaya makes use of large pieces of driftwood and mosses to give the impression of an aged jungle. Red plants highlights adds a sense of vibrancy to the aquascape. Many different plant varietals are used; however, a disciplined approach in demarcating planted and non planted spaces, and open areas, prevents the aquascape from looking overly messy or disorganized.
