# Gabor Vereb | Dutch Style
April 09, 2024 1 min read
This beautiful, densely planted aquascape fits the definition of a Dutch aquascape if one were to follow the rather strict criteria set up by the Dutch Society for Aquarists (NBAT). Note the absence of wood and rocks, with main attention being the immense variety of plant forms in the freshwater environment.
