Boulenger's featherfin tetra, Bryconaethiops boulengeri, is a large alestid characin from the Congo river basin and Lake Tanganyika. Rarely imported, it is a peaceful shoaling species for a community of larger fish, and is farmed commercially for food in its native countries. It grows to 250 mm SL.
## Distribution
Edit
Native to the Congo river basin in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of Congo, Republic of Congo and Zambia. It's also found in Lake Tanganyika.

## Maintenance
Write a note
Edit
It requires a good flow of water through the tank, and decent oxygenation. The choice of decor isn't critical, but a substrate of sand with some driftwood branches, and perhaps some smooth rocks can look very effective. Try to leave as much open swimming space as possible.

## Diet
Write a note
Edit
Omnivorous. Offer a mixture of live, frozen and dried foods. Prawns and live earthworms are particularly relished.
## Behaviour and compatibility
Write a note
Edit
Peaceful with anything that isn't too small as to be considered food. Best kept with other similarly-sized characins, robust cichlids, Loricariids and other bottom dwellers such as Polypterus species. It's a shoaling species so buy a group if possible.
