The giant bumblebee catfish, Pseudopimelodus bufonius, is distributed throughout northeastern South America, from Venezuela to eastern Brazil. Its range extends from the Lake Maracaibo basin, and it grows to 245 mm SL. The type locality is uncertain but probably Cayenne, French Guiana.
## Etymology
Edit
Pseudopimelodus: from the Ancient Greek ψευδής (pseudes), meaning 'false, lying, untrue', and the generic name Pimelodus.
bufonius: 'toad-like'.
## Distribution
Edit
Distributed throughout northeastern South America from the Lake Maracaibo basin in Venezuela to eastern Brazil.
Type locality is uncertain but probably Cayenne, French Guiana.
