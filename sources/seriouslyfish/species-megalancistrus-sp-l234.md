L234, the dragon hi fin pleco, is a large Megalancistrus catfish known only from the rio São Francisco system in eastern Brazil. The L-number was previously applied to Megalancistrus parananus, but that species is native to the Paraná, Paraguay and Uruguay drainages, so L234 appears to be an unidentified species. It grows to 500 to 600 mm and can behave very aggressively in confined surroundings.
## Etymology
Edit
Megalancistrus: from the Greek μέγας (mégas), meaning 'big, large, great', and the generic name Ancistrus.
## Distribution

## Diet
Write a note
Edit
Wild specimens are likely to feed primarily on aquatic invertebrates alongside smaller quantities of wood as in the congener M. parananus, although it's unclear whether the latter is an essential part of the diet or ingested unintentionally while the fish are foraging.
In the aquarium it does best when offered a varied diet comprising sinking dried foods, frozen Daphnia, mosquito larvae, chironomid larvae (bloodworm), and prawn/shrimp, for, example, plus fresh fruit, parboiled potato, etc.
Home-made foods using a mixture of natural ingredients bound with gelatin are very useful since they can be tailored to contain fresh vegetables, Spirulina and meatier ingredients.

## Notes
Edit
L234 was previously applied to M. parananus, which has also has the L-number L113 assigned to it.
However M. parananus is native to the Paraná, Paraguay and Uruguay drainages, whereas L234 is collected in the rio São Francisco so appears to represent an unidentified species.
M. barrae and M. sp. 'LDA097' are also exported from the São Francisco system.
