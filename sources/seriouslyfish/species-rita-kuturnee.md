The Deccan rita, Rita kuturnee, is a bagrid catfish from large river systems in India, growing to around 260 mm SL. Rita species are not often seen in the hobby. Relatively peaceful, it suits communities of larger species such as the bigger barbs, sturdy cichlids and loricariids, although smaller fish will be eaten.

## Maintenance
Write a note
Edit
The decor used is not critical but the fish must be provided with some hiding places. Driftwood pieces arranged to form shelters, flowerpots and lengths of pvc piping can all be used. You will see it more often if the surface is covered in floating plants to cut down the light reaching the substrate.

## Behaviour and compatibility
Write a note
Edit
A robust but relatively peaceful catfish that is an ideal bottom dweller in communities of larger species. Smaller fish will be eaten, however. Good tankmates include some of the larger barb species, such as Tinfoil or Lemon-finned, robust cichlids and loricariids. It can be maintained in groups in larger aquaria, but provide each fish with a refuge to call home.
