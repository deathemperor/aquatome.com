The vundu, Heterobranchus longifilis, is a clariid catfish widespread throughout Africa and one of the largest freshwater fishes on the continent. Growing to 150 cm, it is highly predatory, must be kept alone, and suits only public aquaria or a hobbyist able to house it properly. Its very long adipose fin separates it from similar members of the genus.

## Maintenance
Write a note
Edit
Decor is not critical. Bearing in mind the adult size of this fish an undecorated tank with a soft substrate is probably the best option. It could also be kept in a suitably-sized tropical pond. Filtration should be very efficient as it produces a lot of waste.

## Diet
Write a note
Edit
A greedy and omnivorous species that will eat almost anything offered. A varied mixture of dried pellets, meaty frozen foods and vegetable matter is recommended. Adult specimens do not need to be fed every day. Take care not to overfeed as this is one fish that just doesn't know when to stop eating!
## Behaviour and compatibility
Write a note
Edit
Highly predatory and should be kept alone. It will consume any fish it can fit in its mouth, and will often attack larger tankmates as well.

## Notes
Edit
One of the largest freshwater fish species found in Africa, this is not a very good aquarium inhabitant. It is suitable only for the truly dedicated hobbyist able to house it properly or for public aquaria. It can be distinguished from similar members of the genus by its very long adipose fin.
