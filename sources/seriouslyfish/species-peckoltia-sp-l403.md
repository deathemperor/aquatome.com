L403 is an undescribed wood-eating suckermouth catfish in the genus Panaqolus, known only from the rio Anapu in Pará state, Brazil. The species is xylophagous, feeding primarily on wood, so driftwood must be provided. It should be given a mainly vegetarian diet, with meaty foods kept to a minimum. It has been bred in aquaria.
## Etymology
Edit
Panaqolus: a diminutive form of the generic name Panaque.
## Distribution
Edit
Known only from the rio Anapu, a tributary of the lower Amazon basin in Pará state, Brazil.

## Maintenance
Write a note
Edit
Not difficult to maintain under the correct conditions; we strongly recommend keeping it in a tank designed to simulate a flowing stream with a substrate of variably-sized rocks, sand, fine gravel, and some water-worn boulders.
This can be further furnished with driftwood branches, terracotta pipes, plant pots, etc., arranged to form a network of nooks, crannies, and shaded spots, thus providing broken lines of sight.
Driftwood in particular is highly-recommended since this species feeds on wood (see 'Diet').

## Diet
Write a note
Edit
This species feeds mainly by rasping wood, so it must be given a selection of driftwood in its aquarium. Proprietary dried foods made for wood-eating fishes can be offered as well.
It should be given a mainly vegetarian diet, with meaty foods kept to a minimum.[1][2]
