### Key Takeaways
- The article provides an alphabetical list of fish with names starting with "S", including their common and scientific names.
- Some fish, like the Serpae Tetra and Siamese Fighting Fish, are popular in aquariums, but they have specific requirements and behaviors that need to be considered.
- The list offers insights into the varying sizes and care needs of different fish species, emphasizing the importance of suitable tankmates and environments.
There are countless fish species worldwide, many seen in lakes and streams, and several are popular aquarium pets like the Sharp-Toothed Tetra and Striped Sleeper Goby. They often go by their common names, but knowing the scientific Latin name can aid research. Here's a list of fish names beginning with 'S', arranged by common name alongside their scientific names.
List of Aquarium Fish Species by Common Name
## Serpae Tetra
Hyphessobrycon eques. This is part of the group of tetras known as the "blood tetras," due to its color. This species, from Brazil and Paraguay, grows to only about 1.75 inches, is a peaceful and social fish, and is very easy to care for.
Although it requires tankmates, avoid placing it with smaller fish as it may become slightly aggressive and nip, especially during feeding. Originating from the Amazon, it prefers dark waters—using a dark substrate in the tank can enhance its comfort.
## Siamese Fighting Fish
Betta splendens, commonly referred to as a betta, is one of the most popular aquarium species. Its name comes from the males' tendency to spar when placed together—mixed genders are fine, but avoid housing two males in the same tank. Although often kept in small bowls, they prefer more humane conditions with at least two gallons of water.
Rarely getting more than 3 inches long, Betta spendens doesn't live more than two or three years in normal circumstances, but the bright color and long flowing fins of the males still make this a very popular fish.

## Silver Gourami
Trichogaster trichopterus, also known as blue gourami, three-spot gourami, or golden gourami, is a popular aquarium fish. It grows to about four inches and is easy to care for. Typically a silvery blue, it darkens during spawning.
Gouramis are highly social but should be kept with fish of a similar size, as they can be territorial and may bully smaller fish.
## Serrated Piranha
Serrasalmus serrulatus is a large, distinctive fish that serves as a conversation piece in aquariums. Growing up to 15 inches, it requires a spacious tank (20 gallons or more) with well-circulated water. Being aggressive, it should only be kept with fish of similar size or larger.
The Serrated Piranha consumes a variety of foods, including vegetables and aquarium plants. Its tank should be decorated with caves and other hiding spots.
