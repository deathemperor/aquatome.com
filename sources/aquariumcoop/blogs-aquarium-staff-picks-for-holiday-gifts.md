If you’re searching for the perfect gift for the fish hobbyist in your life, look no further than this handy gift-buying guide from Aquarium Co-Op. To ring in the holiday season, we asked some of our friends and employees about their favorite Co-Op merch and essentials, and this is what we learned: Aquarium Co-Op Hoodie Jimmy's pick Whether it’s a run to the grocery store or to the backyard to check on his ricefish breeding ponds, Jimmy is probably wearing his Aquarium Co-Op hoodie. It’s light enough for warmer weather but also cozy enough to be worn on chilly, rainy days – a frequent occurrence in the Pacific Northwest. This soft sweatshirt is easy to wash as well, when you inevitably dip your sleeves in aquarium water for the millionth time. To quote Jimmy: “Yeah, my Co-Op sweatershirt is really comfortable. I like that it has the big, white Aquarium Co-Op logo on the front. Sometimes people will come up to me at the store if they recognize me from our YouTube videos.” Die Cut Decal Stickers Randy's picks If there was an award for the most Aquarium Co-Op decals on a laptop, Randy would probably win it. He says, “Having these stickers designed and made makes me happy, and I certainly love slapping them all over my laptop. However, seeing the community's enthusiasm for these stickers makes me even happier!” These decals not only look awesome, but they will hold up for a long time because of their durability and water resistance. Join Randy and the community by taking a photo of the unique place you put your sticker and sharing it on our forum or in a review on the website. Murphy Mesh Back Hat Cory's pick If you’re looking for the perfect combination of sun protection, style, and the ability to cover up a bad hair day, the Murphy mesh snapback hat from Aquarium Co-Op does it all. With Murphy’s charming smile featured right on the front, it’s no surprise that Cory wears this hat so often. The back of the hat features the classic trucker mesh style for ultimate air flow. According to Cory, “I love this hat for summer because it’s got all the mesh in the back and is super comfortable. The snapback is adjustable, so it'll fit both small and large heads like mine. Love these hats.” Aquarium Co-Op Mug Candi's pick No matter where she goes, Candi always has a delicious and healthy beverage in hand. Staying hydrated is so important. At home, her green mug from Aquarium Co-Op is her favorite way to enjoy a hot cup of tea while relaxing in her beautiful garden or working hard to answer customer questions. It features the Aquarium Co-Op logo and favorite slogan in white on each side and holds 13.5 oz of your favorite hot or cold drink. It’s even safe to use in the microwave. “I don’t drink coffee, but my Aquarium Co-Op mug is just perfect for a hot cup of tea in the morning. Plus, it washes up really easily too. I love it!” she says. Aquarium Co-Op T-Shirts Irene's picks If you’ve ever seen Irene on the Co-Op YouTube channel, you might have noticed that she wears an Aquarium Co-Op black t-shirt in every video. Whether it’s a short commercial or a long, informative video, Irene is always sporting this comfortable cotton tee while working with her aquariums. She loves it because it matches well with any outfit. Additionally, it’s durable and super easy to toss in the washer and dryer when it gets dirty from tank water and filter gunk. The Co-Op black t-shirts are available in two styles: a goldfish in all red and a Mbu puffer in blue and yellow. Classic Black Aquarium Co-Op Hat Robert's pick There is nothing like a basic black accessory to complete an ensemble. And when you manage a fish store like Aquarium Co-Op, you need a durable hat to hold up during long shifts while moving hundreds of fish around. This is exactly why Robert loves his black hat from Aquarium Co-Op. This fitted black cap does the job well for the long haul, all while looking sporty and professional. Plus, it has FlexFit technology, making it comfortable to wear all day long without squeezing your noggin. Robert says he finds the black Co-Op hat especially handy when it rains (which is almost every day in Washington) because the nice long brim keeps the rain away from his glasses. Aquarium Co-Op Measuring Glass and Spoon Lizzie's picks Did you know Aquarium Co-Op makes a measuring glass and spoon? It’s true, and they’re quite handy for about a million different uses. Whether it’s measuring Ich-X, salt for brine shrimp, plant fertilizer, or anything else under the sun, the Aquarium Co-Op measuring tools get the job done. “The spoon has a really nifty design. It’s super compact and has the ability to measure ¼ teaspoon, ½ teaspoon, 1 teaspoon, and 1 tablespoon. It’s sorcery. I love the Co-Op measuring glasses too. I even propagate plant cuttings in some of mine. They’re cheap and useful, and they would make fantastic stocking stuffers,” says Lizzie.
