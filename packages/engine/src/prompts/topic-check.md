On topic (never return these):
- background, causes, mistakes, tools and equipment needed to do what the title is about
- related subjects that change the answer to the title (for a lighting-hours guide: CO2, plants' needs, algae from too much light, choosing a light)

Off-topic: a section whose subject would be its own guide, unconnected to the title's question (lighting hours → pre-filter cleaning; a betta breeding guide → choosing a canister filter).
