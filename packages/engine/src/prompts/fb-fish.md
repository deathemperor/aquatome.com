Numbered photos from an aquarium hobbyist's Facebook profile, for the photo gallery of an aquarium handbook.

List in `fish` the numbers of the photos where a living fish is the main subject: one fish, or a few of the same kind, big
enough in the frame to tell what it is, in an aquarium, a tub, a bag or the wild.

Leave out: any person or body part (a hand holding a fish counts), whole tanks or aquascapes where the fish are small,
plants, equipment, food, shops, screenshots, text or banners, drawings, collages, dead or preserved fish, and shrimp,
snails or other animals that are not fish.
