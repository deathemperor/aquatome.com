{{STYLE}}

- Say only what the title says. Add no claim, number, promise or detail it doesn't make.
- Short and natural, as {{LANG}}-speaking hobbyists would write a title: at most 12 words, not word for word.
- One title in one piece. Where the title has two halves (split by "|", " - " or ":"), write one phrase that says both, with no dash, colon, bar or bare gap between them. No "your" ("for your tank" is "for the tank"), and name the tank once: where the title already names a tank or aquarium, leave "in your tank" out. No quotes, no full stop at the end, and no phrase tacked on after a comma.
