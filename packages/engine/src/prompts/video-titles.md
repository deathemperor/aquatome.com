{{STYLE}}

- Say only what the title says. Add no claim, number, promise or detail it doesn't make.
- Short and natural, as {{LANG}}-speaking hobbyists would write a title: at most about 70 characters, not word for word.
- One title in one piece. Where the title has two halves (split by "|", " - " or ":"), write one phrase that says both, with no dash, colon, bar or bare gap between them (in Thai, a space between the halves is a gap too). Where one half only says again what the other says, keep the one that says more, and make what only the other adds (who it's for) part of its sentence. A series' part number goes last.
- Keep each word's strength, in the words a keeper would type in their own video title, not a dictionary's or a news headline's: a wow stays a wow, with no "very" added. Keep a describing word joined to what it describes, never left alone after a bracket.
- Capitals only where {{LANG}} writes them (the first word, a name, a brand), not on every word as an English title has them.
- Where the title names this exact fish or plant, use the name given in brackets as it is written there; a scientific name in the title names it, so write that name, then the scientific name in brackets. Where the title names only a wider group ("Pleco", "Betta", "Cichlid" for any of them), write the group's word as the name in brackets writes it, without what makes it this one kind, in lower case where {{LANG}} writes a common noun so: one page calls one fish by one word. Keep a brand, a product's model or a scientific name as it is; a scientific name after a common name goes in brackets.
- Keep sizes as the title gives them. After gallons or inches, add litres or centimetres in brackets, rounded ("40 gallon (about 150 litres)"), written as {{LANG}} writes numbers.
- Keep "planted" as the hobby's {{LANG}} word for a tank with live plants (Vietnamese "bể thủy sinh", Thai "ตู้ไม้น้ำ", Indonesian and Malay "aquascape"), never the English word "planted". That word stands for the whole tank, so no other word for tank beside it.
- Leave out hashtags, emoji, shouting capitals, exclamation marks, and words asking the viewer to watch or subscribe. No "your" ("for your tank" is "for the tank"), and name the tank once: where the title already names a tank or aquarium, leave "in your tank" out. No quotes, no full stop at the end, and no phrase tacked on after a comma.
- If the title says almost nothing, only a code or a channel's name, write only the name in brackets. Never invent what the video shows.
- If what you'd write is only a scientific name, begin it with the {{LANG}} word for fish or plant, so the title is never left with no {{LANG}} word in it.
- One item per title, its n the title's number.
