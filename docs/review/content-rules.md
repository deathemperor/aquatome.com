# Content rules

Generated by `pnpm engine audit` every engine cycle: rules every published page is checked against, in Vietnamese,
Indonesian and Thai (`packages/engine/src/content-rules.ts`, docs/governance.md). A rule never holds a page back. What
a rule can fix safely is fixed whenever the engine writes a page, and on every page each cycle before the build
(`pnpm engine sweep`; the real-content test holds those rules at zero); the rest is listed here, each page once per rule.

Pages checked: 4140 (Vietnamese 2573, Indonesian 787, Thai 780). Imperial units
beside a metric figure: feet 2, gallon 8.

## Numbered headings broken or miscounted (1)

A heading with no number between two numbered ones that follow each other ("#3 …", "Xtreme Betta Pellets", "#4 …") becomes its item's bold lead line when the engine writes the page (listed until then); a number skipped or repeated is listed.

- [vi · Ánh sáng, CO2 và dinh dưỡng: Vì sao cây thủy sinh khó phát triển](https://aquatome.com/huong-dan/anh-sang-co2-va-dinh-duong-vi-sao-cay-thuy-sinh-kho-phat/) — numbers go 5 twice

## Title promises a count the text doesn't have (0)

A title with a number ("10 loại cây…", "Top 5…") whose text has no run of numbered headings or labels, no list or bold leads of that many, and not enough headings for that many items. The sweep has the title rewritten (reviewer-gated), else drops the number from it; the address stays.

- none

## Empty headings and blocks (0)

A heading at the end of its text or over one that isn't its sub-section (an empty one, a group, or its peer: "Ngày 16…" over "Ngày 26…", "2. …" over "3. …"), an empty block: the sweep drops them. A heading straight over its sub-sections is a group, and stays: the site shows it a level above them.

- none

## A heading twice on a page (0)

The same heading twice on one page. Listed: which of the two sections to keep is a person's call.

- none

## The source site's comments or related posts (0)

The source page's furniture, as a heading or a paragraph of its own ("4 bình luận về…", "7 ý kiến về…", "Để lại bình luận", "Leave a Reply", "Bài viết liên quan:"): other readers' names and replies, a comment form, links to the source's other posts. The sweep cuts it, with the list of related posts or everything up to the next heading (more than half a text is listed under the next rule).

- none

## Comments longer than the text (0)

The source page's comments or related posts, more than half of a text: the sweep leaves such a text as it is. Cut by hand, or the text written again.

- none

## Numbered labels out of order (0)

Labels such as "Sai lầm 1", "Mẹo 2", "Bước 3" whose numbers neither count up nor down ("Sai lầm 1, 9, 2, 3…"): the sweep numbers them again in the order they appear.

- none

## Text naming a video, a channel or a reviewer (1)

A page written from videos (a video guide, a product's reviews) whose text names one of its channels or talks about the video or who made it ("trong video", "người review", "theo tác giả"): the page's source list carries that. Listed; a product's review summary never publishes with one (product-reviews.ts).

## A product AquaTome doesn't control, said to be controlled (0)

A product page saying AquaTome controls it, when the code doesn't (@aq/schema controlOf: the Chihiros lines the light tool drives, the plug makers the app switches). The page's control block comes from the code; running text doesn't get to promise it.

- none

## A paragraph repeated on the page (0)

The same paragraph (60 characters or more) twice on one page: in two of its texts, as a run of copies, or 120 characters or more (a short line a list repeats under each item is the text's own). The sweep drops the later copy.

- none

## Sentences left in English (0)

A sentence with no word of the page's language and English's small words (the, and, of…), outside reference lists, citations and quotes.

- none

## Vietnamese left on Indonesian and Thai pages (0)

Letters only Vietnamese uses, outside the Vietnamese names the site marks as such.

- none

## Imperial figures (0)

A figure in °F, inches, feet, gallons or pounds (`pnpm engine metric` converts fish, plant, breed and guide pages before each build). Imperial figures beside a metric one are counted at the top.

- none

## A photo caption naming another fish, a map or an ad video's poster (0)

A species or plant photo whose caption names another species the site has a page for, and not the page's, with nothing saying the fish is still the page's; or says it is a map, or is an ad video's title ("video of: …"). The sweep takes the photo off the page; a map or an ad's poster leaves every page (engine-state/_photo-rejected.json), a photo of another fish only this one, so it may still join that fish's page. Checked on the Vietnamese page; the photos are the same in every language.

- none

## Data card values outside sane bounds (0)

A figure outside the bounds the engine keeps when it reads a page (temperature 4–40 °C, pH 2–10.5, GH 0–50, size 0.3–300 cm, tank 10–1000 cm, difficulty 1–8, lifespan 0.2–150 years), a range upside down, or a minimum tank shorter than twice the adult fish. The sweep hides the figure from the card (kept in the page's file, marked `hidden` with why). Checked on the Vietnamese page; the card is the same in every language.

- none
