# Content rules

Generated by `pnpm engine audit` every engine cycle: rules every published page is checked against, in every language
the site serves (`packages/engine/src/content-rules.ts`, docs/governance.md). A rule never holds a page back. What
a rule can fix safely is fixed whenever the engine writes a page, and on every page each cycle before the build
(`pnpm engine sweep`; the real-content test holds those rules at zero); the rest is listed here, each page once per rule.

Pages checked: 7044 (Vietnamese 4109, Indonesian 1347, Thai 1340, Malay 248). Imperial units
beside a metric figure: feet 1, gallon 14.

## Numbered headings broken or miscounted (2)

A heading with no number between two numbered ones that follow each other ("#3 …", "Xtreme Betta Pellets", "#4 …") becomes its item's bold lead line when the engine writes the page (listed until then); a number skipped or repeated is listed.

- [vi · Ánh sáng, CO2 và dinh dưỡng: Vì sao cây thủy sinh khó phát triển](https://aquatome.com/huong-dan/anh-sang-co2-va-dinh-duong-vi-sao-cay-thuy-sinh-kho-phat/) — numbers go 5 twice

## Title promises a count the text doesn't have (0)

A title with a number ("10 loại cây…", "Top 5…") whose text has no run of numbered headings or labels, no list or bold leads of that many, and not enough headings for that many items. The sweep has the title rewritten (reviewer-gated), else drops the number from it; the address stays.

- none

## A name not as hobbyists write it (0)

A species', plant's or breed's Vietnamese name with a place in lower case ("Rêu java" beside "Dương xỉ Java"), or a plant's name with "Cây thủy sinh" before it, which a list of plants says already ("Cây thủy sinh Vallisneria (cỏ lươn)"). The sweep writes it as hobbyists do (alt-names.ts tidyName) and keeps the address; a name a person fixed is left.

- none

## Empty headings and blocks (0)

A heading at the end of its text or over one that isn't its sub-section (an empty one, a group, or its peer: "Ngày 16…" over "Ngày 26…", "2. …" over "3. …"), an empty block: the sweep drops them. A heading straight over its sub-sections is a group, and stays: the site shows it a level above them.

- none

## The source site's comments or related posts (0)

The source page's furniture, as a heading or a paragraph of its own ("4 bình luận về…", "7 ý kiến về…", "Để lại bình luận", "Leave a Reply", "Bài viết liên quan:"): other readers' names and replies, a comment form, links to the source's other posts. The sweep cuts it, with the list of related posts or everything up to the next heading (more than half a text is listed under the next rule).

- none

## Comments longer than the text (0)

The source page's comments or related posts, more than half of a text: the sweep leaves such a text as it is. Cut by hand, or the text written again.

- none

## A fish called by the name it no longer has (0)

A sentence that gives a fish's old name beside its genus or its English name ("cá bút chì (Nannostomus)"). Since 2026-11-07 "cá bút chì" is the Siamese algae eater and the pencilfish "cá chì", the flat hillstream loaches "cá tỳ bà bướm", and Jordanella floridae has no "cá cờ" in its name. A sentence that also has the new name, or names the fish the old name is now ("cá bút chì (Crossocheilus)"), may be right and isn't listed. The sweep writes the name when the sentence says which fish it is (renameSwapped); what it leaves is listed, and the real-content test holds it at zero: the page is written again with the name in process.md's list.

- none
