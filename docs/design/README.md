# Design specs

What a screen should look like once the owner approves it, with the mockups the builder works from. A builder's
screenshots (light, dark, the largest text) are checked against these before the PR merges.

- [The account sheet and Bể của tôi](profile-sheet.md) (iOS)
- [Tìm, the handbook's home](tim.md) (iOS)
- [The owner's dashboard](dashboard.md) (web and iOS)
- [The photo grid](photo-grid.md) (iOS and web)

The mockups are HTML at iPhone 17 Pro size (402×874 points), with SF Symbols exported from macOS. Their text comes from
the app's strings or is marked as a draft for the builder to word, and their content from the site's app index.
